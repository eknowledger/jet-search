# Lisbon Trip Log

## Day 1 — Alfama

Landed at 10:40 and took the metro straight to the flat near Graça. Spent the afternoon getting lost in Alfama: steep alleys, laundry over the streets, and a view from the Miradouro de Santa Luzia that made the climb worth it. Dinner was grilled sardines at a place with six tables. Heard fado for the first time at a tiny bar later; nobody talked during the songs.

## Day 2 — Belém

Tram 15 to Belém. The queue for the monastery was long, so we went to the bakery first and ate pastéis de nata warm, with cinnamon. Walked along the river to the tower and back. Very windy.

## Day 3 — Ferry to Cacilhas

Took the ferry across the Tagus to Cacilhas for lunch, then the lift up to the Cristo Rei statue. The light on the city in the evening was the best moment of the trip.

## Practical

- The Viva Viagem card works on the metro, trams and ferries.
- Comfortable shoes are not optional: the pavements are polished limestone and slippery when wet.
- Most places close the kitchen around 22:30.
