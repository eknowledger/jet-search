# Sourdough Bread

A plain country loaf. It takes about 24 hours from feeding the starter to slicing, but only 30 minutes of actual work.

## Ingredients

- 100 g active starter (fed 6–8 hours earlier, bubbly and doubled)
- 375 g water, lukewarm
- 450 g strong white flour
- 50 g wholemeal flour
- 10 g salt

## Method

1. Mix the starter and water, then add both flours. Rest 45 minutes (autolyse).
2. Add the salt with a splash of water and squeeze it through the dough.
3. Bulk ferment 4–5 hours at room temperature, with four sets of stretch-and-folds in the first two hours.
4. Pre-shape into a round, rest 20 minutes, then shape tightly and put it seam-side up in a floured basket.
5. Cover and leave in the fridge overnight.
6. Heat the oven to 250 °C with a Dutch oven inside. Bake the bread covered for 20 minutes, then uncovered at 230 °C for another 20–25 minutes, until deep brown.
7. Cool for at least an hour before cutting. Cutting early makes the crumb gummy.

## Notes

- Hydration is 75%. Drop the water to 350 g if the dough feels unmanageable.
- A longer cold proof gives a more sour flavour.
- This is the loaf I bring when friends ask for "the bread".
