# Journal — Rainy Sunday

Rain all day, the steady kind that makes the flat feel smaller. I meant to go for a long walk and managed twenty minutes before giving up and coming home soaked.

Spent most of the afternoon rewriting my résumé. It had grown into a list of everything I have ever done, so I cut it to one page and kept only the work I would happily talk about for ten minutes. Felt lighter afterwards, oddly. I'll ask Mira to read it this week.

Called Mum in the evening. She's planning the garden for next spring and wants to plant more tomatoes than any two people could eat. We talked about visiting in November.

Picked up fresh bread and a sourdough loaf from the market on the way back, which made up for the walk.

Things I'm grateful for today:

- a dry jumper
- Mum's laugh
- finishing something I'd been avoiding

Tomorrow: back to the beta work, and finally reply to Nadia about the release dates in the [[Q4 Project Plan]].
