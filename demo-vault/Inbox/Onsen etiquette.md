What I've read so far (check again before we go):

- Wash properly at the little seated showers first. The bath is for soaking, not washing.
- No swimsuits.
- The small towel never goes in the water. People fold it and put it on their heads.
- Long hair tied up.
- Some ryokan have private baths you can book for yourselves (kashikiri).
- Get in slowly; get out before you feel dizzy, not after.
