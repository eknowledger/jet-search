A woman in Alfama keeps every umbrella left behind on the 28 tram. The drivers drop them at her door; she labels each one with the date and the stop and hangs them in the stairwell of her building, hundreds of them, like bats.

One day a man comes to claim one, eleven years later, and he knows exactly what is in the inside pocket.

Short story, 3,000 words at most. Something to write when [[The Lighthouse Keeper]] is stuck. Maybe told by the neighbour downstairs, who has never once seen her leave the building.
