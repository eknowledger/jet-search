# Feedback from Rui

Sunday 2025-08-24, after dinner. I read the first full draft aloud to [[Rui]] in the living room, standing up, glass of water as a fake champagne flute. He timed it: **6 minutes 20**. He listened to the end without saying anything, which is how I knew it was too long.

## What he said

1. **"The ferry story is the speech. Everything else is in the way."** The bike story, the toast-restaurant story and the singing in the car all go. He's right, and I'm still a bit sad about the toast restaurant.
2. **"You rush when you're nervous."** I read the funny lines faster, which kills them. → Mark the pauses on the cards. Wait for the laugh at "in charge of the tickets."
3. **"Half the room doesn't know who Bernardo is to you."** The Cork side has never met me. → One line at the start: who I am. And say "That was Bernardo" clearly, on its own, looking at him.
4. **"Drop the Portuguese pun."** The joke about *bilhete* and *bilhete de identidade* only lands with our parents. → Cut. Portuguese only for the opening and the toast.
5. **"Don't end on thanks."** I had a list of people to thank at the end. → End on the toast. The parents will thank everyone.
6. **"Say the grandmother line to the grandmother."** Bernardo's grandmother is the one who told him to be patient with Sofia — look at her at "someone who is not in a hurry."
7. **"The bookmark is the best joke. Don't hurry it."**

## What I changed

| Draft 1 (2025-08-10) | Final (2025-09-01) |
|---|---|
| 6:20, about 890 words | 4:05, about 560 words |
| four stories | one story |
| opens with a quote about love | opens with "Boa noite a todos" and who I am |
| ends with thank-yous | ends with the toast |
| the ticket "turned up later" | the ticket found as a **bookmark** — Rui's favourite detail, made bigger |

## What I didn't change

- He wanted me to cut "thirty years of material" as too cheeky. Kept it. It got a laugh.
- He suggested a joke about Bernardo's sunburn. No.

## For next time

Rui is an excellent audience for exactly one reason: he is honest and bored easily. Read everything important to him first.
