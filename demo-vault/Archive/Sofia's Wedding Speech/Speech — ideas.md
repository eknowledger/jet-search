# Speech — ideas

Brain dump, July 2025. Everything that came to mind for [[Sofia]]'s wedding speech, before choosing. ✔ = used in the [[Speech — final draft]].

## Stories

- ✔ **The lost ferry ticket** — Horta to Pico, 2018. Bernardo gives her his ticket and takes the next ferry. Found three days later, as a bookmark. *This is the speech.* Everything else is decoration.
- Sofia learning to ride a bike on the Foz promenade at seven, steering straight into a stall selling sweets. Cute, too long.
- The summer she ran a "restaurant" for the neighbours from our parents' balcony and charged 50 cents for toast. Funny but only for family.
- Bernardo's first family dinner: four bowls of caldo verde, then admitted he doesn't like kale. → cut, he'd die.
- Sofia singing in the car. Every car. Every trip. Badly. → one line maybe? (Cut by Rui — see [[Feedback from Rui]].)
- The time she sent me a voice message from the top of Pico at 5 am, crying at the sunrise. Lovely, but it's *her* story to tell.

## Lines I like

- "Sofia was in charge of the tickets." — repeat it, it's the joke.
- "Someone who is not in a hurry." ✔ — the heart of it
- "Please don't use it as a bookmark." ✔
- "She found something better than a ticket." — too neat? Softened in the final version.
- "Thirty years of material." ✔

## Structure

1. Who I am (one line)
2. The promise not to embarrass her (one line)
3. The story
4. What it says about Bernardo
5. What I love about Sofia
6. The toast

## Toast options

- "To Sofia and Bernardo — may you always have one spare ticket." ✔ (+ "and someone happy to wait for the next boat")
- "To the next boat." — too cryptic for the Irish side
- "To lost things and found people." — a bit much

## Things to avoid

- Exes. Obviously.
- Inside jokes that need explaining.
- Reading from the phone.
- Going over five minutes. (The first draft was over six.)
- Thanking everyone — the parents will do that.
