# Sofia's Wedding Speech

**Done.** [[Sofia]] married Bernardo on **Saturday 2025-09-06** at a quinta outside Amarante, and I gave the sister's speech after dinner. About **4 minutes**, built around one story: **the lost ferry ticket** from our Azores trip in 2018, the day they met.

## The brief (from Sofia, June 2025)

- "Short. Funny. Don't make me cry before the dancing."
- No exes, no school photos, nothing about the year she dyed her hair green.
- English, because half of Bernardo's family were flying in from Cork — with a Portuguese opening and toast for our side.

## How it went

- Hands shaking for the first line, fine after the first laugh (at "Sofia was in charge of the tickets").
- The ticket, framed, was on table four. People went to look at it all evening.
- Bernardo's grandmother cried at "someone who is not in a hurry". So did Bernardo.
- Sofia hugged me and said "you made me cry before the dancing". Then danced until three.

## What I learned about speeches

1. **One story** beats five anecdotes.
2. **Read it aloud** to someone honest. Writing for the ear is different — shorter sentences, repeat the key line.
3. **Time it.** About 140 words a minute when nervous, even when you think you're slow.
4. **End on the toast.** Not on thanks, not on a joke.
5. Cue cards, big font, numbered. Card 3 fell on the floor and the numbers saved me.
