# First week in Lisbon

A short diary of the first week after the move from Porto. Written on the terrace on most evenings, on my phone, with a glass of something.

## Saturday 15 June — moving day

Up at 5:30. Mudanças Douro arrived at 6:55 and the flat was empty by 10:30. Rui, Pimenta and I took the Alfa Pendular from Campanhã; Pimenta yelled for the first hour and then slept. Santa Apolónia is ten minutes' walk from the flat, which felt like a sign.

The truck reached Alfama at 15:30. The sofa didn't fit in the lift. The movers carried it up six floors and I felt terrible. Done by 19:00. Grilled sardines at the arraial still standing two streets away from Santo António — paper plates, bread, a plastic cup of wine, the whole street singing. A good first night.

## Sunday 16 June

Unpacked the kitchen. Pimenta spent the whole day under the bed and would only come out for food. Fermento survived the trip in its jar; fed it 1:5:5 and it doubled by evening. The first loaf in the new oven was flat — the oven runs cold.

## Monday 17 June

No internet yet; phone hotspot and patience. Requested the address change on the citizen card. Found the market. Bought a basil plant and a rosemary for the terrace — the first two pots of what became the [[Rooftop Garden]].

## Tuesday 18 June

First run in Lisbon: down to the river, along to Cais do Sodré and back. 8 km, very hot, very happy. The hill home at the end is going to make me strong or kill me.

## Wednesday 19 June

Fibre installed, 1 Gbps. The technician drilled through 60 cm of 1930s wall and said "these buildings, eh". Rui set up the Wi-Fi; the bedroom gets almost nothing through the walls. (Mesh system ordered the next week — see [[Internet and Wi-Fi]].)

## Thursday 20 June

Found **the** bakery, on the corner below. Pastel de nata still warm at 8:00. Pimenta came out from under the bed and sat on the window sill for an hour, watching the gulls.

## Friday 21 June

Longest day of the year. Sunset from the terrace: the river going gold, the ferries crossing to Cacilhas, the castle lit up behind us. I took a roll of Portra 400 of nothing but light and roofs. Started a list of what to grow up here.

## Weekend

[[Sofia]] came down on the train on Saturday, brought a lemon tree in a pot "for the terrace", and slept on the mattress in the second bedroom between boxes of books. Sunday: the Carmo ruins, a long lunch, the 28 tram back up because we were too full to walk.

---

What I want to remember: the flat smelled of old wood and grilled sardines, and it felt like ours by Wednesday.
