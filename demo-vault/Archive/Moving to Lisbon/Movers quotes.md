# Movers quotes

Porto → Lisbon, one-bedroom flat plus a lot of books, mid-June. Three companies came to look (or did a video call) in early May 2024. **Chosen: Mudanças Douro, €1,450.** Booked 2024-05-14.

## The quotes

| | Mudanças Douro | Atlântico Mudanças | Furgão Express |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | **€1,450** | €1,980 | €1,100 |
| Crew | 3 + truck | 4 + truck | 2 + van |
| Packing | fragile items packed by them (kitchen, frames) | full packing | none |
| Furniture | taking apart and putting back together the bed and wardrobe | yes | bed only, extra €80 |
| Insurance | up to €10,000 included | up to €15,000 included | basic, €2,000, extra cost for more |
| Date | 2024-06-15 confirmed | only 2024-06-22 | 2024-06-15 |
| Lift / stairs | asked about floors, priced the 6th floor | asked | didn't ask |
| Reviews | very good, a few "late" | very good | mixed — two mention broken glass |
| Written quote | yes, itemised | yes | a text message |

## Why Mudanças Douro

- The date we needed. Atlântico was lovely but a week later, and we had the Porto flat only until the 30th.
- Proper insurance included. Furgão's €350 saving disappears with one broken lens.
- They asked about the stairs in Alfama and the narrow street, and sent a smaller truck to shuttle the last 200 m.
- An itemised written quote. Furgão sent "1100 ok?" by text.

## How it went

- Arrived in Porto at 6:55, loaded by 10:30, in Alfama by 15:30.
- Carried the sofa up six floors when it didn't fit in the lift, without a word of complaint. Tipped them €60.
- Nothing broken. One box of film negatives went missing for an hour — it was in the truck cab.
- Final bill: **€1,450**, exactly the quote.

## If I did it again

Same company. Book even earlier: by mid-May they only had two June Saturdays left.
