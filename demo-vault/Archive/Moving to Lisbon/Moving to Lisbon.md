# Moving to Lisbon

**Done.** We moved from Porto on **2024-06-15** — a Saturday, three days after Santo António. Movers: **Mudanças Douro, €1,450**. [[Rui]], me, [[Pimenta — the cat]], 61 boxes and a sourdough starter in a jar.

## Why

Rui grew up in Lisbon and had missed it for ten years. I wanted a city where I could run along the river every morning and live without a car. Porto will always be home too — [[Sofia]] is there — but three hours on the train is close enough.

## What went well

- Booking movers six weeks ahead — in June everyone moves.
- The "open first" box: kettle, coffee, sheets, cat food, toilet paper, phone chargers.
- Taking the train with Pimenta instead of putting her through seven hours in the truck.
- Photos of every room in Porto on the last day. The deposit came back without a single question.

## What I'd do differently

- Measure the lift **before** moving day. The sofa went up six floors by the stairs.
- Look harder at the windows at the viewing (see [[Bedroom window leak]]).
- Change the address on the citizen card earlier — the confirmation letter takes over a week.
- Fewer books. No, that's a lie.

The flat became [[Home]]: the terrace turned into the [[Rooftop Garden]], a Raspberry Pi turned into the [[Home Server]], and the tired kitchen, two years later, into the [[Kitchen Renovation]].
