# Address changes

Who needed the new Alfama address after the move on 2024-06-15, and when it was done. The good news in Portugal: changing the address on the **Cartão de Cidadão** updates several public records at once (tax, the health service, the electoral roll).

## Public

| Who | How | Done |
|---|---|---|
| Cartão de Cidadão | online with the Chave Móvel Digital; a letter with a code arrives at the new address, enter it on the portal to confirm | requested 2024-06-17, letter 2024-06-26, confirmed 2024-06-26 ✓ |
| Finanças | automatic from the citizen card — checked on the portal | 2024-06-28 ✓ |
| Health service (SNS) | automatic, then registered at the new health centre in person | 2024-07-03 ✓ — still no family doctor, on the waiting list |
| Electoral roll | automatic | ✓ |
| Pimenta's microchip (SIAC) | the vet in Lisbon updated it at the first visit | 2024-07-05 ✓ |

## Lessons

- Do the citizen card first. Half the other places ask for proof of address, and the confirmed card is the easiest proof.
- The CTT redirection was worth every cent — the bank letter and two birthday cards came through it.
- Keep this list. Next time it takes an afternoon instead of a month.
