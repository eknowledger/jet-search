# Apartment hunting — Alfama

Three weekends of viewings in April 2024, staying with Rui's parents. We looked mostly in Alfama, Graça and Mouraria — old, hilly, close to the river.

## What we wanted

- Two bedrooms (one for guests, books and the sewing machine)
- **Up to €1,200** a month
- **Cats allowed** — this ruled out more than anything else
- Light. Porto had been dark.
- Some outdoor space for plants
- Walking distance to the river for running

## Viewings

| Date | Where | Rent | Notes | Verdict |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| 2024-04-06 | Rua dos Remédios, Alfama, 2nd floor | €1,050 | dark, damp smell in the bedroom, no outdoor space | no |
| 2024-04-06 | Graça, near the miradouro | €1,250 | beautiful light, landlord said no pets | no |
| 2024-04-13 | Mouraria | €980 | above a bar; the agent visited in the morning for a reason | no |
| 2024-04-13 | Santa Apolónia | €1,180 | new build, lift, clean, no soul | maybe |
| 2024-04-20 | Alfama, 1st floor | €900 | one bedroom, really — the second was a cupboard | no |
| **2024-04-27** | **Alfama, top floor with roof terrace** | **€1,150** | 1930s building, small lift, private terrace on the roof, 18 m², south-west; original terracotta tiles in the kitchen; cats fine | **yes** |

## The one

We knew at the door to the terrace. Six floors up, the whole river in front of us, the wind trying to take the agent's papers. Rui said "it's windy", I said "it's the river", and we both meant yes.

- Kitchen: tired, but the floor tiles are beautiful, 20 × 20 cm, from the 1930s.
- Bedroom: big window facing south. (*Did not ask what happens when the rain comes from the south. See [[Bedroom window leak]].*)
- Landlord: Sr. Almeida, older, slow to answer but fair. Wanted two months' deposit and a guarantor letter.
- Lease signed 2024-05-10, keys 2024-05-24.
