# Tokyo — Hotel Sakura Asakusa

**4 nights**, check-in Mon 2027-03-29, check-out Fri 2027-04-02. **¥88,000** for the room (¥22,000 a night), double, non-smoking, no breakfast. Pay at the hotel; free cancellation until 7 days before.

## Why this one

- Asakusa is quieter at night than Shinjuku or Shibuya, and close to the river.
- Asakusa station has the Toei Asakusa line, which runs straight from Haneda (the Keikyu trains carry on through). No changes with luggage after a 20-hour trip.
- Walking distance to Sensō-ji and Kappabashi kitchen street.
- Reviews keep saying "small but spotless" — the room is 16 m², which in Tokyo is a palace.

## Around the hotel

- Sensō-ji at night — nearly empty after 21:00, still lit.
- 天丼 at 大黒家, five minutes away.
- The Sumida river path towards the Skytree — a good easy run at sunrise if the legs want it.
- Kappabashi for knives and ceramics. Some shops close on Sundays; we're there Tuesday to Thursday, fine.
