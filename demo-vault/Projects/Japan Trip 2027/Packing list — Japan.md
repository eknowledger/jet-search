# Packing list — Japan

One cabin bag + one 23 kg bag each. The big bags get forwarded twice, so the day pack has to survive alone for 24 hours.

## Documents and money
- [ ] Passports (both valid well past 2027 — checked)
- [ ] JR Pass order confirmation (exchange at Tokyo station on 04-02)
- [ ] Printouts: [[Tokyo — Hotel Sakura Asakusa]], [[Kyoto — ryokan Mori-ya]], [[Osaka apartment]]
- [ ] Travel insurance policy PDF
- [ ] Two cards (one Visa, one Mastercard), ¥60,000 cash in total
- [ ] Coin purse — Japan runs on ¥100 and ¥500 coins

## Clothes (16 days, laundry in Osaka)
- [ ] 6 T-shirts, 2 long-sleeve
- [ ] Warm layer — April nights in Kyoto and Kanazawa can drop to 5 °C
- [ ] Light rain jacket
- [ ] 2 trousers, 1 pair of running shorts
- [ ] Socks **without holes** — shoes come off at the ryokan, in temples, in some restaurants
- [ ] Shoes that slip on and off easily
- [ ] Running shoes + 2 running outfits (easy runs only, three weeks after the [[Half Marathon]])

## Camera bag
- [ ] Fujifilm X-T5 + 23 mm f/2, 3 batteries, charger
- [ ] Two SD cards
- [ ] Olympus OM-1 + 6 rolls of Portra 400, in a clear bag for hand inspection
- [ ] Lens cloth, small blower
- [ ] The "Lisbon Gold" settings written down — see [[Photography]]

## Tech
- [ ] Phone with an eSIM bought and installed before leaving
- [ ] Power bank (cabin bag only)
- [ ] Plug adapter — Japan is type A, 100 V; laptop and camera chargers are fine
- [ ] Earplugs and eye mask for the flights

## Health
- [ ] Vitamin D, 2,000 IU — one a day, enough for 16 days ([[Blood test 2026-09]])
- [ ] Ibuprofen, plasters, blister patches
- [ ] Sunscreen
- [ ] Calf raises 3×15 don't stop on holiday ([[Strength and mobility routine]])

## Small but important
- [ ] Hand towel / handkerchief — many toilets have no paper towels
- [ ] A small rubbish bag — almost no bins on the streets
- [ ] Gifts: tawny port and a Lisbon tile for [[Kenji Mori]]
- [ ] Empty foldable bag for what comes home (the knife, okonomiyaki sauce)

## The day before
- [ ] Online check-in
- [ ] Pimenta's food and the spare key to [[Sofia]]
- [ ] Deep watering on the terrace, timer checked — [[Rooftop Garden]]
