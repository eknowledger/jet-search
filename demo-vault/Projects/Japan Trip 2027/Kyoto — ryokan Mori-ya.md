# Kyoto — ryokan Mori-ya

**3 nights**, 2027-04-02 → 2027-04-05, **¥126,000 with dinner** (kaiseki every night, breakfast too). Host: [[Kenji Mori]]. Booked by email; deposit of ¥30,000 paid 2026-10-04.

Small — six rooms — in a side street in Higashiyama, ten minutes' walk from Yasaka shrine and Maruyama park. Found it through a photo of the garden, of all things: a single maple in a courtyard the size of our kitchen.

## The room

- Tatami; the futons are laid out while we're at dinner.
- Window onto the garden.
- Shared hinoki (cypress) bath, booked by the half-hour; we asked for 21:30.

## Dinner

Kaiseki at 18:30, served in the room. Kenji asked about allergies or dislikes — answer: none, we eat everything, raw things included.

## Etiquette notes to self

- Slippers off before the tatami. Toilet slippers stay in the toilet.
- Wash before getting into the bath; the bath is for soaking only.
- Yukata: left side over right. Right over left is for the dead.
- No tipping. A small gift from Lisbon instead — a bottle of tawny port and a tile from the Feira da Ladra.

## Kenji's tips so far

> "Go to Fushimi Inari before the sun. After nine, you will see only backs."

- Shirakawa canal at dusk on arrival day.
- Maruyama park at night — but eat first, the stalls are expensive.
- He can lend bicycles if the weather is good.
