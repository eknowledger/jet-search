# Kanazawa — maybe

Two nights, **2027-04-09 → 2027-04-11**, after Osaka. It's in the plan as a maybe: **cut if tired**. By then we'll have done Tokyo, Kyoto, Naoshima, Hiroshima and Osaka in eleven days — there's an honest chance we just want a quiet bed.

## For

- **Kenroku-en**, one of the "three great gardens" of Japan, likely in full bloom when the south is already done — Kanazawa runs about four days behind Kyoto ([[Cherry blossom forecast]]).
- Higashi Chaya — the old teahouse district, quieter than Gion.
- Ōmichō market: 海鮮丼 for lunch.
- The 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art — Rui's pick, for Leandro Erlich's swimming pool.
- The Hokuriku shinkansen goes straight back to Tokyo (about 2 h 30), so it's not a detour on the way home.

## Against

- The JR Pass has ended by then — single tickets, roughly ¥9,000 each to get there and ¥14,000 each back to Tokyo.
- Two more train days in a row.
- Fifteen nights in six different beds is already a lot.

## Plan B — no Kanazawa

Back to Tokyo on 04-09 on the Nozomi. Two slow nights in Kuramae, next to Asakusa: maybe an onsen afternoon, Kappabashi for the knife, long breakfasts. Last night at Haneda either way.
