## Notes

- Food at ¥10,000 a day for two holds if breakfast is konbini or bakery and one meal a day is a set lunch. The Kyoto dinners are inside the ryokan price, which helps a lot.
- If Kanazawa is cut, the €174 moves to the Plan B nights in Tokyo — about the same.
- Biggest risk is the yen. All the yen lines add up to ¥633,000; at ¥150 to the euro that is about €290 more. The buffer wouldn't cover it — the knife goes first.
- Cash: ¥60,000 in total to start, the rest by card.
