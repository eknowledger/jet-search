# Japan Trip 2027

**2027-03-28 → 2027-04-12**, 16 days with [[Rui]], timed for the cherry blossoms. Tokyo, Kyoto, Naoshima, Hiroshima and Miyajima, Osaka — and Kanazawa if we still have legs. It falls three weeks after the [[Half Marathon]], so the race is done and the trip is the reward.

## Must-dos

1. Fushimi Inari at 6 am, before the crowds.
2. [[Naoshima art island]] — one full day, ferry from Uno.
3. A sushi counter in Tsukiji outer market — breakfast on day 3.
4. Hanami in Kyoto: the [[Cherry blossom forecast]] puts full bloom around April 3.
5. [[Hiroshima and Miyajima day trip]].
6. [[Kanazawa — maybe]] — cut if tired.
