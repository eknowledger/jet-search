# Flights

Booked **2026-09-20**: Lisbon → Tokyo Haneda via Istanbul, Turkish Airlines, **€1,140 per person** return, economy, one checked bag of 23 kg each. The booking email is in the "Japan" mail folder (reference ends in …LM).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lisbon → Istanbul | Sun 2027-03-28 | 13:05 | 19:55 | 4 h 50 |

## Why this one

- Watched prices for two weeks. The options via Frankfurt or Paris were €1,300–1,450 each.
- The Istanbul layover outbound is long (5 h 35), but at a sane hour for dinner.
- Haneda instead of Narita: 45 minutes to Asakusa on one train.
