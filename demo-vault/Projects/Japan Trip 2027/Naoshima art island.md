# Naoshima art island

**One full day — Tuesday 2027-04-06**, from Okayama, with the ferry from **Uno**. Tuesday because the Chichu Art Museum, like most of the island, closes on Mondays.

## Getting there

1. JR Uno line, Okayama → Uno, about 1 h (JR Pass).
2. Ferry Uno → Miyanoura port, about 20 min (cash, roughly ¥300 each way).
3. E-bikes at Miyanoura — the hills between the port and the museums are steep. Reserve, or be first in line.

## Notes

- Chichu: no photos inside. Monet's water lilies in daylight, the Turrell room, Walter De Maria's sphere. Go slow; it's the reason for the whole detour.
- Timed tickets are the one thing that can ruin the day — reminder set for the sale date.
- Cash for the ferry, the bikes and the small cafés.
- If it rains: fewer outdoor pieces, longer in Chichu. Still worth it.
