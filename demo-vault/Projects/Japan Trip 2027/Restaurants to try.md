# Restaurants to try

Japanese names first — easier to show a taxi driver or match a sign. ★ = must, ☆ = if we're near.

## 東京 Tokyo

| | Name | What | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ★ | 築地場外市場の寿司カウンター | 寿司 (sushi) counter breakfast | Tsukiji outer market | the must-do; すしざんまい本店, or whichever small counter is full of locals |
| ★ | 大黒家天麩羅 | 天丼 (tempura on rice) | Asakusa, by the hotel | the queue moves fast |
| ☆ | つな八 | 天ぷら (tempura) set | Shinjuku | sit at the counter |
| ☆ | 思い出横丁 | 焼き鳥 (yakitori) | Shinjuku | one drink, lots of smoke |
| ☆ | 月島もんじゃストリート | もんじゃ焼き | Tsukishima | Rui wants the "ugly okonomiyaki" |

## 京都 Kyoto

| | Name | What | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ★ | 森屋 (Mori-ya) | 懐石料理 (kaiseki) | the ryokan | three dinners included |
| ★ | 南禅寺 順正 | 湯豆腐 (simmered tofu) | Nanzen-ji | lunch after the Philosopher's Path |
| ☆ | 錦市場 | 卵焼き, 豆乳ドーナツ, pickles | Nishiki market | go hungry |
| ☆ | 中村藤吉 | 抹茶 (matcha) parfait | Kyoto station branch | before the train |
