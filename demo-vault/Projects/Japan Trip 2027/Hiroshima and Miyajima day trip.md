## Things to check

- **Tide table** for 2027-04-07. High tide = the torii "floats"; low tide = walk out to its base. Either is good; just know which one we'll get.
- The museum is heavy. The afternoon stays soft on purpose — nothing booked straight after.
- Miyajima on a weekday in early April: busy but bearable. The last ferries are much later than we need.

## Food

- お好み焼き (広島風) — layered, with noodles. お好み村 has many small counters over several floors; or みっちゃん総本店.
- 焼き牡蠣 — grilled oysters from a stall on Miyajima's Omotesandō.
- あなごめし (conger eel on rice) — うえの at Miyajimaguchi. Famous, so probably a queue.
- もみじ饅頭 — maple-leaf cakes for the train to Osaka.

More in [[Restaurants to try]].

## Photography

Only the X-T5 and the 23 mm today, nothing heavy. No photos inside the museum. The torii at golden hour if we're running late — then we take the next train, no drama.
