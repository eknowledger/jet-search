# Osaka apartment

**2 nights**, Wed 2027-04-07 → Fri 2027-04-09. **¥31,000** in total, paid in full (non-refundable — it was the cheapest by far).

A small apartment in Nippombashi, between Namba and Kuromon market: one room, a proper bed, a tiny kitchen and — the real reason — a **washing machine**. Day 12 is laundry day.

## Arrival

- We get in late (~20:30) from Hiroshima. Self check-in with a key box; the code comes by message 3 days before.
- Big bags forwarded from Okayama by takkyūbin the evening before. The host confirmed they can take deliveries.
- Nearest station: Nippombashi (Sakaisuji and Sennichimae lines), or 10 minutes on foot from Namba.

## House rules (from the listing)

- Quiet after 22:00 — thin walls.
- Rubbish sorted: burnable / plastics / cans and bottles. Bags under the sink.
- No shoes inside.

## Around it

- Kuromon market for breakfast — tuna, grilled scallops, strawberries.
- Dōtonbori 10 minutes away — たこ焼き at わなか, お好み焼き at 美津の (see [[Restaurants to try]]).
- Den Den Town for second-hand cameras. Just looking. Probably.
- Osaka castle park on the Thursday afternoon.
