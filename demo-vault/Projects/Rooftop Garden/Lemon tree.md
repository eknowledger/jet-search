# Lemon tree

A 'Lisbon' lemon — what else — in a 60-litre pot in the corner of the terrace. [[Sofia]] gave it to us as a housewarming present when we moved in ([[Moving to Lisbon]]). She carried it up all six floors in her arms and has never let us forget it.

## Facts
- About 1.4 m tall now, after two years on the roof.
- 2026: 23 lemons so far, and another flush of flowers in September. The smell on the terrace at night.
- On the [[Drip irrigation]] with 3 emitters; less in winter.
- Citrus feed every two weeks from March to September.

## Problems this year
- **Wind burn**: brown, papery leaf edges on the side facing the river. Moved the pot half a metre into the lee of the water tank.
- **Leaf miner**: silvery trails in the new leaves over summer. Ugly, mostly harmless on an older tree. Picked off the worst.
- **Yellow leaves in March**: hungry for nitrogen after the winter. Fixed with feed and a top-dressing of [[Compost]].
- After the [[Aphids and ladybirds]] release in June, the larvae pupated on it — free guards for the rest of the summer.

## Winter
Lisbon rarely freezes, but the roof is its own climate. For the cold months:
- **Fleece** over the whole tree on nights forecast below 4 °C, and on any night with a storm warning — on the 6th floor the wind chill does more damage than the cold itself. The fleece and a bag of clothes pegs live in the box by the terrace door.
- Water only when the top of the pot is dry.
- No feeding from October to February.

Part of the [[Winter plan — broad beans and garlic]].
