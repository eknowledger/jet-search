# Planting calendar 2026

What happens on the terrace, month by month. Lisbon is mild, but the roof is not: six floors up, facing south-west, the wind takes three or four degrees off a winter night, and the summer sun bakes the pots from eleven until sunset. Dates are this year's real ones where I have them; the rest is the plan.

| Month | Sow indoors | Sow or plant out | Harvest | Also |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| January | — | — | Lemons, parsley | Order seeds from the [[Seed list]]; scrub the pots |
| February | Tomatoes on 15 Feb (San Marzano, Cherokee Purple); chillies the same day | — | Lemons, parsley | Heat mat on the bookshelf, well away from the leaky bedroom window |

## Notes for next year
- Sow the basil later. Both March sowings sulked until May anyway — see [[Herbs — basil, mint, parsley]].
- The tomatoes went out on 20 April and survived, but a cold, windy week right after set them back. Go by the forecast, not the date.
- Order the ladybirds at the end of May, before the aphids find the chillies, not after — [[Aphids and ladybirds]].
- Winter crops are new this year: [[Winter plan — broad beans and garlic]]. If they go well, the tomatoes follow the beans in the same trough in April.
