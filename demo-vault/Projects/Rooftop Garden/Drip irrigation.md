# Drip irrigation

The terrace has one tap, next to the water tank. Everything runs from there.

## The setup
- Tap → battery timer → filter → pressure reducer → 16 mm main line along the foot of the parapet.
- 4 mm micro-tube from the main line to each pot, ending in a **2 L/h** pressure-compensating emitter. Pressure-compensating matters: the far end of the line gets the same as the near end.
- **Timer at 6:30, 12 minutes**, every day from April to October. Early, so the water soaks in before the sun reaches the terrace and the leaves are dry by midday.

12 minutes at 2 L/h is 0.4 L per emitter per day.

| Zone | Emitters | Water per day |
|---|---|---|
| Tomato troughs (2) | 10 | 4.0 L |
| Chilli pots (3) | 6 | 2.4 L |
| Lemon tree | 3 | 1.2 L |
| Parsley and mint | 3 | 1.2 L |
| **Total** | **22** | **8.8 L** |

The basil is *not* on the drip any more — see [[Herbs — basil, mint, parsley]].

## Heat waves
In the August heat waves I **doubled it to 24 minutes**. Pots on a roof get far hotter than anything at street level, and the tomatoes were wilting by 4 pm even with their 12 minutes. Back to 12 at the end of August. On the worst days I also hand-watered the chillies at sunset.

## Checking an emitter (the cup test)
1. Pull the emitter out of its pot and put it in a measuring jug.
2. Run the timer manually for 6 minutes.
3. You should get **200 ml**. Much less means it's clogged.

## Maintenance
- Lisbon water is hard. Every couple of months, soak the emitters overnight in vinegar. A clogged one shows up as a single wilted plant in a row of happy ones.
- Unscrew and rinse the filter once a month in summer.
- New batteries in the timer every October (done 2026-10-11). The power cut on 2026-08-04 didn't touch it — the whole reason for a battery timer.
- Winter: every other day from November; off entirely when it rains for a week.

## Parts, for when something breaks
16 mm pipe, 4 mm micro-tube, 2 L/h emitters, barbed connectors, end caps, a hole punch. Spare emitters are on the [[Seed list]].
