# Rooftop Garden

The terrace on the roof: **18 m²**, facing **south-west**, on the **6th floor**, and **windy** most afternoons. Full sun from about eleven until sunset in summer, which is wonderful for tomatoes and brutal for anything with soft leaves. The wind is the real enemy — everything tall gets staked twice, and the pots huddle in the lee of the parapet and the water tank.

## Systems
- **Water** — [[Drip irrigation]]: 2 L/h emitters, timer at 6:30, 12 minutes. Doubled in the August heat waves.
- **Soil** — [[Compost]] from kitchen scraps and cardboard, browns to greens 3:1 by volume.
- **Pests** — [[Aphids and ladybirds]]: aphids on the chillies in June, 50 ladybird larvae released on 2026-06-14, aphids gone in two weeks.

## The 2026 season so far
- Tomatoes sown indoors on 2026-02-15, planted out on 2026-04-20, first harvest on 2026-07-08. About 14 kg in total.
- The basil finally survived once I stopped drowning it and started bringing it in on cold nights.
- August heat waves: irrigation doubled, shade cloth over the herbs in the afternoons.
- Now, late October: the summer troughs are cleared and the winter crops are in — broad beans and garlic, and fleece ready for the lemon tree. See [[Winter plan — broad beans and garlic]].
