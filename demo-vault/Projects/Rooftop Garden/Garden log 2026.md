# Garden log 2026

The terrace diary. Short entries, typed on my phone on the roof, tidied up later.

### 2026-02-08
Seed tin sorted, trays scrubbed. Bought fine seed compost — the homemade stuff is too rough for seeds.

### 2026-02-15
**Sowed the tomatoes indoors**: 12 cells of San Marzano, 8 of Cherokee Purple. Chillies the same day — Padrón, malagueta, jalapeño, aji amarillo. Heat mat at 24 °C on the bookshelf.

### 2026-02-22
San Marzano up. Cherokee Purple still sulking.

### 2026-03-10
Potted the tomatoes on into 9 cm pots. Sowed basil.

### 2026-03-28
Half the basil tray damped off. Too wet. Again.

### 2026-04-12
Hardening off: tomatoes out on the terrace by day, in at night.

### 2026-04-20
**Planted out the tomatoes**: six San Marzano, four Cherokee Purple in the two long troughs. Wind gusting 40 km/h; two canes each. [[Rui]] held the plants while I tied them.

### 2026-04-26
[[Drip irrigation]] back on: timer at 6:30, 12 minutes.

### 2026-05-03
Basil dead. Plastic pot, saucer full of water, a drip emitter on top of that. Took the emitter off before the next one.

### 2026-05-17
Compost bin too wet — sour smell, slime, fruit flies. Forked in two bags of torn cardboard. Taped the crack in the lid.

### 2026-05-24
New basil, in a terracotta pot this time. Watering only when the top 2 cm are dry.

### 2026-05-31
Compost warm in the middle again. Smells like a forest.

### 2026-06-06
Aphids all over the chilli tips. Ants farming them.

### 2026-06-14
Released **50 ladybird larvae** at dusk on the chillies, a few on the lemon tree. See [[Aphids and ladybirds]].

### 2026-06-28
Aphids gone. Two weeks exactly. Ladybird pupae on the lemon leaves.

### 2026-07-08
**First harvest.** One Cherokee Purple, 310 g. Ate it with Rui on the terrace, salt and olive oil, standing up.

### 2026-07-19
Blossom end rot on the first San Marzano truss. Watering has been uneven — I've been topping up by hand on top of the drip. Stopping that.

### 2026-08-03
Heat wave. 39 °C in the city, worse on the roof. Irrigation doubled to 24 minutes. Shade cloth over the herbs from noon.

### 2026-08-04
Power cut in the whole street for most of the day. The battery timer didn't notice. The freezer did.

### 2026-08-16
Second heat wave. Still on 24 minutes. Hand-watering the chillies at sunset.

### 2026-08-30
Back to 12 minutes. The tomatoes made it.

### 2026-09-06
Passata day: 7 kg of San Marzano → 10 jars.

### 2026-09-20
Saved Cherokee Purple seed (fermented three days, rinsed, dried on a plate).

### 2026-10-04
Pulled the tomatoes. Season total about 14 kg.

### 2026-10-11
Bought garlic and broad bean seed. New batteries in the timer.

### 2026-10-15
Night forecast 11 °C — **basil moved inside** to the living-room window. Not the kitchen: the kitchen is rubble.

### 2026-10-18
Garlic in: 24 cloves, 10 cm apart.

### 2026-10-24
Broad beans sown, two rows. Fleece bought and waiting in the box by the door. See [[Winter plan — broad beans and garlic]].
