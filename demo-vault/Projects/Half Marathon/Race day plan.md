# Race day plan

**Lisbon Half Marathon, Sunday 2027-03-07.** Goal **1:45:00** (4:58 /km). Course notes in [[Race — Lisbon Half Marathon 2027]].

## The day before

- 3 km shakeout + 3 strides in the morning.
- Bib and safety pins collected at the expo on Friday.
- Kit laid out: Pegasus, the blue shorts, singlet, cap, two gels, watch charged.
- Dinner at 19:00: rice, salmon, a little bread. Nothing new. In bed by 22:30.

## Fuel and water

- **Gel at km 8**, **gel at km 15** — the same caffeine-free ones I've been testing on long runs since October.
- **Water every 5 km**: a few sips at each station; walk five steps to drink rather than choke.
- If it's warm (rare in March): extra water at km 10 and 17, some on the neck.

## If things go wrong

- Shin pain: drop to easy pace. If sharp, stop. Japan is three weeks later; no race is worth that.
- More than a minute behind 1:45 pace at km 15: switch to the back-up goal — a PB under 1:53:40 — and enjoy it.
- Side stitch: breathe out hard on the left foot, slow down for 30 s.

## People

- [[Rui]] at km 15 with a spare gel and the loud voice.
- Afterwards: pastéis de nata in Belém, then a long nap.
