## The 2025 race — 1:53:40

What I wrote the evening after:

> Bridge was glorious and freezing. Went through 10 km in 52:40 feeling fine. Took the only gel at km 12, too late. Km 16–18 along the river felt like they would never end; the Belém finish came out of nowhere. 1:53:40 — four and a half minutes off Porto. Happy, but I know where the time is.

What changes for 2027:

- **Two gels**, at km 8 and km 15, not one late one ([[Race day plan]]).
- Real long runs: 19 km twice in February, not one 16 km.
- Tempo and interval sessions instead of "running a bit faster sometimes".
- Strength — which in 2025 meant nothing at all.
