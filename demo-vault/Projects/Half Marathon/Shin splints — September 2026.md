# Shin splints — September 2026

## What happened

- **2026-09-05**: 16 km long run in Monsanto, lots of downhill. Dull ache on the inside of the left shin from km 12. Finished anyway — mistake.
- **2026-09-06/07**: ache walking down stairs in Alfama (which is all of Alfama). Sore to press along the inner edge of the bone, lower third.
- **2026-09-09**: saw [[Inês Costa]]. Medial tibial stress syndrome — shin splints. Nothing pointing to a stress fracture: tender over 6–8 cm rather than one sharp spot, and hopping was uncomfortable but possible.

## Why

The honest list:
1. August mileage went from ~30 to 42 km a week in three weeks.
2. Monsanto hills three Sundays in a row, downhills taken hard.
3. Everything in one pair of shoes, no rotation.
4. Weak calves, according to Inês: "Your calves are lazy and your shins pay for it."

## What Inês prescribed

- **2 weeks rest** from running (2026-09-09 → 2026-09-23). Cycling and swimming allowed if pain-free.
- **Calf raises 3×15 daily** — both legs the first week, single-leg from the second.
- Tibialis raises against the wall, 3×15.
- Ice after an achy day; no stretching into pain.
- Return by walk–run, starting with 1 min run / 1 min walk × 10.

## Lessons

- About 10 % a week. Really.
- Downhills count double.
- Calf raises every day, for good — [[Strength and mobility routine]].
- A second pair of shoes for long runs ([[Shoes]]).
