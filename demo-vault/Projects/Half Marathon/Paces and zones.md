# Paces and zones

Set from the 1:53:40 of 2025 and a solo 10 km time trial in July 2026 (48:50), with the goal of **1:45:00** for the half — **4:58 /km** — as the anchor.

## Paces

| Run | Pace | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Recovery | 6:20+ /km | could sing |
| Easy / long | **5:50–6:10 /km** | full sentences |
| Steady | ~5:25 /km | short sentences |
| Half marathon race pace | **4:58 /km** | a few words |
| Tempo | **5:05 /km** | "comfortably hard", 20–40 min |
| Intervals | **6×800 m at 4:35** /km (3:40 per rep), 400 m jog between | hard but controlled |
| Strides | ~4:00 /km for 20 s | quick, relaxed |

Yes, the tempo (5:05) is slower than goal race pace (4:58) for now. Tempo comes from current fitness; race pace assumes the block pays off. Re-check after the 10 km tune-up in week 8 and adjust.

## Heart-rate zones

Heart-rate reserve method: resting **54 bpm** ([[Resting heart rate]]), max **186 bpm** (seen on hill repeats in Monsanto, May 2026). Reserve = 132.

| Zone | % of reserve | bpm | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Z1 | 50–60 % | 120–133 | recovery, walking hills |
| Z2 | 60–70 % | 133–146 | easy runs, long runs |
| Z3 | 70–80 % | 146–160 | steady |
| Z4 | 80–90 % | 160–173 | tempo, race pace |
| Z5 | 90–100 % | 173–186 | intervals, the last kilometre |

## Adjustments

- Hot or humid: easy runs by HR, not pace. Add 10–15 s/km.
- Wind on the river path: run the effort, ignore the watch on the way out.
- Hills in Monsanto: by effort, and downhills **gently** — that's how the shins went in September ([[Shin splints — September 2026]]).
