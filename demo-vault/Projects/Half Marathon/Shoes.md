# Shoes

## Main pair — Nike Pegasus 41

- Bought 2026-04-12, €140.
- **420 km on them** (as of 2026-10-25).
- **Replace at ~600 km** — the foam goes flat long before the upper looks tired, and the shins notice.
- For the Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday runs.

At 30–40 km a week, 600 km lands somewhere in early January. Buy the replacement in December and run it in for a couple of weeks before the old pair retires.

## Second pair for long runs — ASICS Novablast 4

- Bought 2026-10-03, €130 in a sale.
- Softer, a bit more rocker. Only for the Sunday long runs, so neither pair runs two days in a row.
- First run 2026-10-11; about 30 km so far.

## Why two pairs

After the [[Shin splints — September 2026]], Inês said two things: don't jump the mileage, and don't run every kilometre in the same tired shoe. The science on rotation isn't strong, but the cost is only buying the next pair a bit earlier.

## Race shoes?

No carbon plates for this one. The race will be in a fresh pair of Pegasus with about 100 km on them by March. Nothing new on race day.
