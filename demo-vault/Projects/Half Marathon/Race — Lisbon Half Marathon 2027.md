## The course

It starts on the 25 de Abril bridge — the one day a year you can run across it — with the river far below and, usually, a headwind. Off the bridge into Alcântara, then flat along the river. Long out-and-back stretches, so you see the fast runners coming back, which is either motivating or crushing. The finish is in Belém, near the monastery.

- Elevation: flat apart from the bridge (a gentle climb, then down).
- Wind is the big unknown. North wind on the bridge = a slow first 5 km. Accept it.
- Crowded start: thousands of runners plus the people doing the shorter fun run. Find the right pen.

## Why this race

- It's home. I'll know every lamppost by the end.
- March in Lisbon: 12–17 °C, usually dry. Ideal.
- Three weeks before [[Japan Trip 2027]] — a hard deadline, no "postponing the goal".
- Ran it in 2025 ([[Previous races]]), so I know where it hurts: km 16–18, the long straight.

## Logistics

- Train across the river to the start — leave home at 7:30, the trains fill up.
- Bag drop near the start; bags are taken to the finish.
- No hotel — sleeping in my own bed is the best part of a home race.
- After: find [[Rui]] by the pastry-shop queue in Belém, obviously.
