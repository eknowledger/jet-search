# Strength and mobility routine

From [[Inês Costa]] after the shin splints, adjusted a little since. Two parts: the **daily** bit (five minutes, non-negotiable) and the **full routine** on Mondays and Fridays (about 35 minutes).

## Daily — every day, rest days too, Japan too

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Calf raises** | **3×15** | on the edge of a stair; 3 s down, quick up. Single-leg since the second rehab week |
| Bent-knee calf raises | 2×15 | for the soleus — "the forgotten calf" |
| Ankle circles | 10 each way | while the coffee brews |

## Full routine — Monday and Friday

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Single-leg calf raises with a 6 kg backpack | 3×12 each | load the calves properly |
| 2 | Tibialis raises, back against the wall | 3×15 | the front of the shin — the bit that hurt |
| 3 | Split squats | 3×10 each | quads and balance |
| 4 | Single-leg glute bridge | 3×12 each | strong hips keep the knee from falling in |
| 5 | Side plank | 3×30 s each | |
| 6 | Dead bug | 3×10 | core without crunches |
| 7 | Single-leg balance, eyes closed | 3×30 s each | ankles |

## Mobility — after runs, 10 minutes

- Kneeling hip flexor stretch, 2 × 45 s each side
- Hamstring stretch on the arm of the sofa, 2 × 45 s
- Foam roller on the calves — gently; Inês was clear: "it's not a punishment"
- Couch stretch, 1 min each side, when the quads complain

## Progressions

- 3×15 single-leg calf raises got easy → added the backpack (October).
- Tibialis raises: resistance band added in base week 2 ([[Week 2 log]]).
- From January: step-ups and some skipping drills.

## Keeping the habit

- September: 21 of 22 days after the diagnosis
- October so far: 24 of 25 days (one missed Saturday in [[Week 1 log]])

The routine lives on a card taped inside the bathroom cabinet door. Pimenta likes to sit on the mat during the side planks.
