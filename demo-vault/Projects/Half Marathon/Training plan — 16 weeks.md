# Training plan — 16 weeks

From **Monday 2026-11-16** to race day, **Sunday 2027-03-07**. **4 runs a week**, long run up to **19 km**, peak week **52 km** (week 14). Every fourth week is lighter. Paces are in [[Paces and zones]].

## Weekly shape

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | strength + mobility ([[Strength and mobility routine]]) |
| Tue | quality: intervals or tempo |
| Wed | rest, or 30 min on the bike |
| Thu | easy run with strides |
| Fri | short strength session |
| Sat | easy run (sometimes with Rui, very easy) |
| Sun | long run |

## Rules

- Weekly volume up by about 10 % at most, except coming out of a lighter week.
- Shin pain above 3/10 the next morning → the next run becomes a rest day; if it's still there after two days, message Inês.
- Long runs at easy pace, always. Race pace only in the last 5 km of week 11 and in weeks 12 and 15.
- Sleep before mileage: under 6 h, and the quality session moves.
- Calf raises 3×15 every day, plan or no plan.
