## Where we are

Demolition is finished. The room is back to bare walls, the ceiling and the covered floor. Two surprises, both small:

- A **bricked-up niche** in the wall by the window, about 40 cm wide. Probably an old food cupboard. We keep it open as a shelf for the cookbooks.
- The old wiring behind the wall units was in worse shape than expected: brittle insulation, one junction box taped together. Good that it all goes.

## Electrics

The electrician walked us through the route for the new circuits.

- **Dedicated 32 A circuit for the induction hob** straight from the board
- Separate circuits for oven, countertop sockets, appliances and lights
- New RCD on the kitchen side of the board
- Socket positions marked on the wall in pencil, agreed and photographed

Details in [[Electrics and plumbing]].
