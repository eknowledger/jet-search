# Quote comparison

Three contractors came to see the kitchen in mid-August. Each got the same one-page brief: strip the room, move the sink 60 cm, new electrics with a hob circuit, plaster and paint, keep the terracotta floor, fit cabinets and a countertop that we buy ourselves.

## The three quotes

| | **Ortega & Filhos** | Casa Nova Obras | Reforma Rápida |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quote | **€18,750** | €21,300 | €16,900 |
| What it covers | labour and materials, all but cabinets and countertop | same scope, plus a "coordination fee" | same scope |
| Written guarantee | yes, 2 years on labour | yes, 2 years | **none**, "we always come back" |
| Itemised? | 4 pages, down to the bags of mortar | 1 page, lump sums | a two-line text message, no breakdown |
| Earliest start | 2026-10-05 | mid-November | "next week" |
| Their estimate | 10 weeks | 12 weeks | 6 weeks |
| Site visit | measured twice, looked under the sink, asked about the floor | 20 minutes, tablet photos | 15 minutes |
| Recommended by | neighbour on the 2nd floor | online reviews | nobody |

## Why Ortega & Filhos

1. **Written guarantee and an itemised quote.** I can see exactly what €18,750 buys, line by line.
2. **The middle price.** €2,550 less than Casa Nova Obras for the same scope, with nothing missing.
3. **They can start on 5 October**, which makes a finish on 14 December realistic. Casa Nova would have pushed us past Christmas.
4. **[[Luis Ortega]] looked at the things that go wrong.** He opened the cupboard under the sink, pressed the floor with his heel and said, "we will see when it is open." (We did: see [[Rotten joist under the sink]].)
5. The neighbour's bathroom, which they did last year, still looks perfect.

## Why not the others

- **Reforma Rápida, €16,900**: cheapest, but no written guarantee. With old pipes and an old floor, that is the one thing I won't skip.
- **Casa Nova Obras, €21,300**: nothing wrong, just more money and a later start for the same kitchen.

## Decision

> **2026-08-30: Ortega & Filhos.** Decided with [[Rui]] over dinner on the terrace. Called Luis the next morning; 30% deposit paid, works start 2026-10-05, weekly site meeting Mondays 8:30.
