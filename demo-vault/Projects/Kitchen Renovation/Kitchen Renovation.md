# Kitchen Renovation

> A kitchen we can cook a proper meal in by **14 December 2026**, so Christmas dinner happens at home and not at Rui's aunt's.

## Goal

The kitchen in the Alfama flat had not been touched since the 1980s: gas rings with one dead burner, two sockets (one scorched), a sink jammed in the darkest corner and cupboard doors that came off in your hand. The plan:

- New cabinets from **Nordhaus** in oak veneer
- **Induction** hob instead of gas, on its own 32 A circuit
- Sink moved **60 cm** towards the window
- **Quartz** countertop, "Calacatta Light"
- Keep the **1930s terracotta floor** if at all possible

Contractor: [[Luis Ortega]] of **Ortega & Filhos**. Plumbing: [[Marta Silva]]. Decisions made together with [[Rui]].
