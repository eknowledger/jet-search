## Against the budget

| | |
|---|---|
| Budget | €24,000 |
| Committed | €28,240 |
| **Over by** | **€4,240** |
| Of which surprises (the joist) | €950 |
| Of which "we chose nicer things" | €3,290 |

How it happened: in August we split the €24,000 as roughly €17,100 builder, €3,500 cabinets, €1,000 countertop, €2,400 contingency. Then the real numbers came in. The builder was €1,650 more than the guess, the oak cabinets €2,700 more, the quartz €1,340 more. Those upgrades alone used up the whole contingency and €3,290 more, and the first real surprise, the [[Rotten joist under the sink]] (€950), came on top.

> Decision with [[Rui]], 2026-09-20: keep the oak and the quartz, cover the gap from savings, and **no more upgrades**. Anything new has to replace something else.
