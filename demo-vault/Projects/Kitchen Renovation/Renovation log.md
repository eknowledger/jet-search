# Renovation log

Running log of the [[Kitchen Renovation]], oldest first, written the same evening. Meetings have their own notes; money lives in the [[Budget tracker]].

## August 2026

### 2026-08-02 · Sunday
The cupboard door under the sink came off in my hand again. [[Rui]] screwed it back on and said, for the third time this year, "we should just redo the whole thing." This time I said yes. Sat on the floor with a tape measure and a notebook: the room is 3.9 × 3.6 m, about 14 m², one window onto the alley, gas rings from the 1980s, two sockets (one scorched), a sink in the darkest corner. And the terracotta floor, which is the one thing I love and the one thing I want to keep.

### 2026-08-04 · Tuesday
Power cut across half of Alfama in the afternoon heat. The server went down hard (see [[Power outage 2026-08-04]]). While I was in the hallway with a torch I looked at the fuse board properly for the first time: two kitchen circuits on old ceramic fuses. Whatever else we do, the electrics are part of this.

### 2026-08-06 · Thursday
Money talk with Rui on the terrace. We settled on **€24,000** for everything, with a **10% contingency (€2,400)** inside that. Our guess: around €17,100 for a builder, €3,500 for flat-pack cabinets, €1,000 for a laminate top. Writing it down here so I can laugh at it later.

### 2026-08-11 · Tuesday
Three contractors booked for visits this week. The neighbour on the 2nd floor swears by Ortega & Filhos, who did her bathroom last year. The other two came from online reviews.

### 2026-08-13 · Thursday
[[Luis Ortega]] came at 18:00 and stayed almost an hour. Measured everything twice, asked how old the floor was, opened the cupboard under the sink, sniffed, and pressed the floor with his heel. "We will see when it is open." Promised a written quote within a week.

### 2026-08-14 · Friday
Casa Nova Obras in the morning: a project manager with a tablet, twenty minutes, didn't look under anything. Reforma Rápida after lunch: friendly, fast, could start "next week", which in a kitchen this old sounded more like a threat than a promise.

### 2026-08-19 · Wednesday
Two quotes in. Casa Nova Obras: **€21,300**, one page of lump sums. Reforma Rápida: **€16,900**, but when I asked about a guarantee the answer was "we don't do that on paper, but we always come back." No written guarantee means no.

### 2026-08-21 · Friday
Ortega & Filhos: **€18,750** for labour and materials, everything except the cabinets and the countertop. Four pages, itemised down to the bags of lime mortar, with a two-year written guarantee on labour. Read it twice on the tram.

### 2026-08-30 · Sunday
Decided: **Ortega & Filhos.** Comparison in [[Quote comparison]]. Rui and I agreed over grilled sardines at home that the middle price with a guarantee beats the cheap price with a handshake. Calling Luis first thing tomorrow.

### 2026-08-31 · Monday
Called Luis at 8:00. Works start **2026-10-05**, target finish **2026-12-14**, site meeting every Monday at 8:30. Paid the 30% deposit, €5,625, before lunch.

## September 2026

### 2026-09-03 · Thursday
Hob decision: **induction**, not gas. The three reasons are in [[Decision — induction or gas]]: air quality, the building's old gas pipes, and the €480 gas inspection a new gas hob would need. Rui sulked for an evening about the wok. Bought a €39 portable induction plate so he can try it before the old rings go.

### 2026-09-05 · Saturday
Nordhaus showroom. I walked in thinking flat-pack and walked out in love with **oak veneer**. The planner drew our room in twenty minutes. The price: **€6,200**, lead time **6 weeks**. Long walk home along the river, doing sums.

### 2026-09-10 · Thursday
Rui after a week of portable induction: pasta water boils faster than on gas, the eggs are fine, the wok "is not the same, but okay." Verdict accepted.

### 2026-09-12 · Saturday
Ordered the cabinets from Nordhaus: oak veneer, €6,200, lead time 6 weeks, so delivery around 24 October. Paid half. Order details in [[Cabinets — Nordhaus order]].

### 2026-09-17 · Thursday
The stone yard Luis recommended. Laminate looked sad next to the oak sample. Chose quartz, **"Calacatta Light"**, **€2,340 installed**; templating happens after the cabinets are fitted. Notes in [[Countertop — quartz]]. Added everything up on the tram home: €27,290 before a single surprise. We are over.

### 2026-09-20 · Sunday
Booked the flights for [[Japan Trip 2027]] in the morning, which did not help the money mood in the afternoon. Long talk: we keep the oak and the quartz, cover the gap from savings, and **no more upgrades**. Anything new replaces something else. Written into the [[Budget tracker]].

### 2026-09-26 · Saturday
Lifted a loose tile by the door to see what the floor is laid on: lime mortar over boards, which is good news for keeping it. Found **2 boxes of spare tiles** in the storage room, left by the previous owner, same 20 × 20 cm terracotta. Counted the floor: 14 m², about 350 tiles. [[Floor tiles]].

### 2026-09-28 · Monday
[[Marta Silva]] came to look at the sink move. Moving it 60 cm towards the window is easy, she says; the waste pipe has enough fall. She also didn't like the smell under the sink. That's two of them now.

## October 2026

### 2026-10-03 · Saturday
Emptied the kitchen. Temporary kitchen in the living room: fridge, microwave, kettle, the portable induction plate and a washing-up bowl in the bathtub. Pimenta hates all of it and sleeps on the fridge.

### 2026-10-05 · Monday
**Works start.** First site meeting at 8:30: [[Week 1 — site meeting 2026-10-05]]. Hardboard went down over the floor by 9:15, and by 11:00 the old wall units were in rubble bags at the bottom of the steps. No skip fits in our street, so everything goes down by hand.

### 2026-10-07 · Wednesday
Dust everywhere despite the plastic sheets. The wall tiles came off in big slabs. Behind them, by the window, a **bricked-up niche**, probably an old food cupboard. Luis grinned like a boy.

### 2026-10-09 · Friday
End of week 1. The room is bare walls, ceiling and covered floor. It looks bigger and much older. Good week.

### 2026-10-12 · Monday
[[Week 2 — site meeting 2026-10-12]]. The electrician walked us through the circuits, including the dedicated **32 A** one for the hob, and we marked sockets in pencil on the walls. Marta starts tomorrow. Nordhaus confirmed delivery for Saturday 24.

### 2026-10-13 · Tuesday
Bad day, then a better evening. Marta took out the old sink base, lifted the tiles in the corner and pushed a screwdriver into the joist below. It went in to the handle. Decades of a slow drip from the old waste joint. Luis came over, gave us three options, and recommended sistering a new joist alongside: **€950 extra**. Looked at the photos with Rui at 19:00, texted Luis "yes" at 19:30. [[Rotten joist under the sink]].

### 2026-10-15 · Thursday
Joist repaired. A new treated joist bolted along the old one, new boards, all dry. Luis checked the ceiling of the flat below: nothing. I could stand where the sink used to be and the floor didn't move at all.

### 2026-10-16 · Friday
The 32 A circuit for the hob is in, and the kitchen side of the fuse board is new, with a proper RCD. The old ceramic fuses are in a jar on the bookshelf. Rui wants to keep them "for history."

### 2026-10-19 · Monday
[[Week 3 — site meeting 2026-10-19]]. About two days behind because of the joist. Luis is calm about it; he thinks the crew catches up during the cabinet fitting.

### 2026-10-21 · Wednesday
Marta's pressure test held for two hours. The sink connections now sit **60 cm** along, under the window. Washing up with daylight, finally (in theory, in about a month).

### 2026-10-23 · Friday
First fix of the electrics done, the window wall plastered, the niche squared off. The sink corner tiles are back down in lime mortar, nine of them from the spare boxes. You can only see which ones if you know.

### 2026-10-24 · Saturday
**Nordhaus delivered:** 23 boxes, in the living room as Luis asked. One dented box (the glass wall door), to open on Monday before the crew starts. Pimenta claimed the tall larder box within ten minutes. Cabinet fitting starts after Monday's meeting, and then the countertop can be templated. Seven weeks to the target finish.
