# Rotten joist under the sink

**Found:** Tuesday 2026-10-13, by [[Marta Silva]], when she lifted the tiles in the old sink corner
**Extra cost:** **€950**
**Approved:** the same evening, 2026-10-13
**Repaired:** 2026-10-15

## What we found

Marta had felt the floor give a little when she knelt in the corner during [[Week 2 — site meeting 2026-10-12]]. On Tuesday she took the old sink base out, lifted about 1.5 m² of terracotta and the boards beneath it, and pushed a screwdriver into the end of the joist. It went in up to the handle.

- About 80 cm of the joist end soft and dark, crumbling where it sits in the wall
- The boards above it damp, two of them rotten through
- Cause: the old waste joint under the sink, dripping a little, probably for decades
- The other joists Luis could reach: dry, hard, fine

> Luis, on the phone: "Good that we found it now. In two years the sink would be in the neighbour's kitchen."

## Options Luis gave us

| Option | What | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Sister the joist** | new treated joist bolted alongside, new boards, relay tiles | **€950** | 2 days, no need to touch the flat below |
| B. Replace the whole joist | cut out and replace wall to wall | ~€2,100 | lift another 5 m² of tiles, open the neighbour's ceiling |
| C. Patch and ignore | fill, new boards only | ~€300 | no. |

## Decision

**Option A, €950.** I called [[Rui]], we looked at the photos together at 19:00, and I texted Luis "yes" at 19:30. It goes on the stage 2 invoice. See [[Budget tracker]].

## The repair (2026-10-15)

1. Rotten boards out, joist end cleaned back to sound wood and treated
2. New joist bolted along the full length, with hangers into the wall
3. New boards the same day; lime mortar and the tiles back in their numbered order on 2026-10-23
4. Six tiles had cracked on the way out, so they were replaced from the two spare boxes ([[Floor tiles]])
5. Luis checked the ceiling of the flat below: no stain, nothing to do

## Lessons

- Old flats hide things under sinks. Budget for it.
- The 10% contingency exists for exactly this, even if ours had already gone on oak and quartz.
- The new sink has an isolation valve, a proper trap and a drip tray in the cupboard. Check it once a month.
