# Electrics and plumbing

Weeks 2 and 3 of the [[Kitchen Renovation]] (12–23 October). Everything here is inside the Ortega & Filhos quote except the extra for the joist.

## Electrics

The old kitchen had two circuits on ceramic fuses shared with half the flat. The electrician from Luis's crew replaced the kitchen side of the board and ran new cable to everything.

| Circuit | Breaker | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| **Induction hob** | **32 A, dedicated** | hob only |
| Oven | 16 A | built-in oven in the tall unit |
| Countertop sockets | 16 A | 4 double sockets along the back wall |
| Appliances | 16 A | fridge, dishwasher |
| Lighting | 10 A | ceiling spots, under-cabinet LED strip |

All on a new RCD. The dedicated 32 A circuit is the reason we could choose induction at all (see [[Decision — induction or gas]]).

**Contracted power:** we have 6.9 kVA. With the hob, the oven and the kettle all on at once we could trip the main breaker, so the hob gets its power-management setting turned down a notch. If that annoys us at Christmas, we ask the supplier to raise it.

### Socket positions (agreed 2026-10-12)
- Two doubles either side of the hob, 15 cm above the upstand
- One double at the end by the window for the coffee machine
- One inside the corner unit for the cordless vacuum
- A switched spur above the tall larder for the hood

## Plumbing

[[Marta Silva]] moves the sink **60 cm** towards the window, so you finally get daylight while washing up.

- New hot and cold supply in PEX, with isolation valves under the sink
- Waste pipe extended with a steady fall to the old stack; checked on her visit in September
- Dishwasher on its own valve, so the Bosch can be taken out without shutting off the kitchen
- Pressure test held for 2 hours on 2026-10-21
- Old gas line capped at the meter, labelled

It was Marta who found the problem under the old sink on 2026-10-13: see [[Rotten joist under the sink]].
