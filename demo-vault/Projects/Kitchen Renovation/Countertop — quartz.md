# Countertop — quartz

**Choice:** quartz, colour **"Calacatta Light"**, 20 mm, **€2,340 installed** (templating, cutting, fitting, sink and hob cut-outs, edge polishing).

Supplier: the stone yard [[Luis Ortega]] uses for all his kitchens. They came recommended, and they talk to his crew directly, which means fewer phone calls through me.

## What we looked at

| Material | Rough price for our 4.2 m | Why not / why |
|---|---|---|
| Laminate | ~€600 | the original plan; looks tired in five years, hates water at the joints |
| Solid oak | ~€1,400 | beautiful, but oiling it every few months next to the sink? No |
| Granite | ~€2,100 | too busy next to the oak cabinets |
| **Quartz "Calacatta Light"** | **€2,340** | pale, quiet veining, no sealing, survives red wine and turmeric |

## How it happens

1. Cabinets fitted, level and fixed (see [[Cabinets — Nordhaus order]])
2. **Templating after cabinets**: they make a full-size template on site
3. About 10 days at the stone yard
4. Fitting in one morning; sink is undermounted the same day
5. Marta connects the tap and waste afterwards (see [[Electrics and plumbing]])

So the countertop date depends entirely on the cabinet fitting. If the base units are in by early November, the top goes in mid-November and we still have a month to spare.

## Details agreed

- Undermount sink, single bowl, drainer grooves cut into the stone on the right
- Hob cut-out to the hob's template (bring the paper template from the box)
- Upstand 10 cm along the back wall, same quartz
- Eased edge, no bullnose

## Care notes for future me

> Quartz is **not** heat-proof. Hot pans from the oven go on a trivet or the hob, never straight onto the top.

- Daily: warm water and a drop of washing-up liquid
- No bleach, no oven cleaner, no scouring pads
- Cut on a board; the quartz will blunt the knives before the knives scratch the quartz
