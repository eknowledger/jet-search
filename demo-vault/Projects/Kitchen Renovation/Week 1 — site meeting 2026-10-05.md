## Notes

**Schedule.** Luis repeated the plan: **demolition in week 1**, **plumbing and electrics in weeks 2–3**, cabinets as soon as they arrive (Nordhaus said around 24 October), countertop templating after the cabinets, then tiles, plaster and paint. **Target finish 2026-12-14.** He thinks we have about a week of spare time "unless the building surprises us."

**House rules.**
- Noisy tasks 9:00–13:00 and 14:00–18:00 on weekdays only; notice for the neighbours is up in the stairwell
- One key for Luis, one for the crew; nobody needs us home
- Pimenta stays shut in the bedroom on site days, with a sign on the door

**Floor.** Hardboard over the whole floor, taped at the joins, before anything comes off the walls. Nobody lifts a tile without asking first. See [[Floor tiles]].

**Debris.** No skip fits in our street, so everything goes down in rubble bags, carried to the van at the bottom of the steps. That is in the quote.

**Shut-offs.** Water off for a couple of hours on Tuesday when the old sink comes out; temporary cold tap in the bathroom. Power to the kitchen circuits off from Wednesday, the rest of the flat stays on.
