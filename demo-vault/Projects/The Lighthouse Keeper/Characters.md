# Characters

## The lighthouse
- **[[Elena Varga]]** — the keeper's daughter, 19. Wants to leave for Lisbon. Has been secretly keeping the night watch since the spring.
- **[[Tomás Varga]]** — the keeper, her father, 56. Going deaf, and hiding it.
- **Amélia Varga** (1919–1953) — Elena's mother, dead of pneumonia when Elena was ten. Counted the ninety-six steps with her. Once had a ticket to Lisbon and burned it in the stove. Only ever seen through other people.

## The ship
- **Henrique** — radio operator on the supply ship *Santa Luzia*, 24, from Ponta Delgada. Talks too fast; listens to the whole Atlantic on his set at night. Brings the mail ashore every second Thursday so he can see Elena. He is the door out — and he is aboard when the ship goes down in chapter 12.
- **Captain Bettencourt** — master of the *Santa Luzia*. Owes Henrique favours. Barely on the page.

## The village (Fajã das Garças)
- **Dona Graça** — the village midwife, about 68. Has delivered 412 children and remembers the weather for each one. Narrator of chapter 7, the only chapter in first person. Notices everything; it's her trade. She was the one who told Amélia to go.
- **Padre Anselmo** — the priest. Rings the bell at the Elevation, which is how Graça found out about Tomás. Puts the inspector up every October.
- **Zé do Vigia** — the old whale lookout, Tomás's oldest friend. Fires the rocket when he sees a spout.

## Away
- **Tia Lurdes** — Amélia's older sister. Left at seventeen; a seamstress in Alfama, Lisbon. Writes in a round convent hand. Has a room and a nursing-school place waiting for Elena.
- **Senhor Nogueira** — the lighthouse inspector. Comes every October. Eats all the priest's chouriço.

## Who wants what

| Character | Wants | Fears |
|---|---|---|
| Elena | Lisbon; a life where nobody needs her to listen for them | that her father falls apart the day she leaves |
| Tomás | to keep the light until he can't | being retired to a house with no sea in the window |
| Henrique | Elena, and a bigger radio | being sent to Angola |
| Graça | to have been right about Amélia | that she wasn't |

## Names to keep straight
- The lighthouse: Ponta Negra. The village: Fajã das Garças. The ship: *Santa Luzia*.
- Elena calls her father *Pai*. Graça calls Elena *filha*. Nobody calls Tomás anything but Tomás or *o faroleiro*.
