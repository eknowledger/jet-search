# Outline — three parts

Three parts, 21 chapters, about 80,000 words. September 1962 to the spring of 1963. **Fog** is the lie, **Storm** is the cost, **Light** is the choice.

## Rules for the whole book
- Close third on Elena everywhere except chapter 7.
- Every part opens with a weather and closes with a light.
- The deafness is never named by Tomás. Other people name it; he doesn't.
- Time between Parts Two and Three passes the way it does in [[To the Lighthouse]] — quickly, in the house, without explanation.
