# The Lighthouse Keeper

My first novel. Literary fiction, set on **Flores, in the Azores, in 1962**: a lighthouse on the western cliffs, a keeper who is going deaf, and his daughter, who wants to leave for Lisbon and can't, because she's the only one who knows.

## Where it stands
- **Goal: 80,000 words. At 31,500 words on 2026-10-20.**
- Three parts — **Fog**, **Storm**, **Light** — and **21 chapters**. **Chapters 1–9 drafted.**
- Next up: chapter 10, then the big one — [[Chapter 12 — notes for the storm]].
- The habit: 600 words a day before breakfast, Sunday revision — [[Writing habit]].

## The people
- [[Elena Varga]] — the keeper's daughter, 19, wants to leave for Lisbon.
- [[Tomás Varga]] — the keeper, her father, going deaf.

## Questions I keep coming back to
- Does Henrique survive the storm? (See the chapter 12 notes.)
- Does it end with Elena in Lisbon, or Elena in the tower? I know which one is braver to write. I don't know yet which one is true.
- Title: is *The Lighthouse Keeper* about Tomás or about Elena? The answer should change as you read.
