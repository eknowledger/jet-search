# Unclog a sink

From the gentlest fix to the messiest. Most of the time the bathroom sink is hair and soap, and the kitchen sink is grease and coffee grounds. In this building the drains are old, so: no chemical drain cleaners, ever.

## 1. Hot water

A kettle of hot — **not boiling** — water, poured slowly. Boiling water can soften the PVC joints under the sink. Wait five minutes. If it starts draining, a second kettle.

## 2. Plunger

- A small cup plunger, not the toilet one.
- **Block the overflow hole** with a wet cloth (bathroom sink), otherwise the pressure just escapes.
- A few centimetres of water in the basin, so the plunger seals.
- Ten firm up-and-down pumps without lifting it off, then pull it away sharply.

## 3. Baking soda and vinegar

Half a cup of bicarbonate, then a cup of vinegar, plug in, wait 30 minutes, then hot water. Honestly it does very little for a real clog, but it helps with smells and light grease.

## 4. The trap (sifão)

The U-shaped bend under the sink catches most things.

1. Bucket and old towel underneath.
2. Unscrew the two plastic nuts by hand (anticlockwise looking from above).
3. Empty it into the bucket. Rinse it in the bath, not the sink you just took apart.
4. Check the rubber washers are seated, screw it back on hand-tight.
5. Run the tap for a minute and check for drips.

## 5. Drain snake

For the bathroom: a cheap plastic hair-grabber strip with barbs (€3 at the hardware shop on Rua dos Remédios). Push it down, twist, pull out. Disgusting and very effective.

For the kitchen: a hand auger, fed in through the pipe after taking the trap off.

## When to stop and call someone

- More than one drain is slow at the same time — the problem is further down, in the building's pipe.
- Water comes **up** in another sink or the shower.
- The trap is clear and it's still blocked.

Call [[Marta Silva]]. She also did the trap the time I cross-threaded it.

## Prevention

- Strainer in the kitchen sink. Coffee grounds into the compost for the [[Rooftop Garden]], not down the sink.
- Grease wiped from pans with paper first.
- Hair catcher in the shower.
- Once a month: a kettle of hot water down each drain ([[Cleaning routine]]).
