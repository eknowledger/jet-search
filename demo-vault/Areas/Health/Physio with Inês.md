# Physio with Inês

Sessions with [[Inês Costa]], physiotherapist, for the shins and the build-up to the [[Half Marathon]]. Her clinic is in Graça — 15 minutes' walk uphill (good warm-up, bad cool-down).

## 2026-09-09 — assessment

- Left shin, inner edge, lower third. Tender over 6–8 cm, no single sharp point.
- Diagnosis: medial tibial stress syndrome — shin splints. Hop test uncomfortable but possible; nothing to suggest a stress fracture, so no scan for now.
- The cause, in her words: "Too much, too soon, too downhill."
- Plan: **2 weeks rest** from running. **Calf raises 3×15 daily.** Tibialis raises. Bike or swim if pain-free.

## 2026-09-23 — follow-up

- Press test: almost nothing. Hop test pain-free.
- Cleared for walk–run: 1 min / 1 min × 10, every other day, then build.
- Calf raises go single-leg; add the bent-knee version for the soleus.
- To keep doing: ice if it aches, no hills for a month, no running two days in a row yet.

## 2026-10-14 — check

- Running again since 09-24, up to 8 km, no pain. Calves stronger — 3×15 single-leg with a backpack now.
- Gait check on the treadmill: a slight overstride, heel landing far ahead. Cue: "quieter feet". Cadence from ~162 towards ~170 steps a minute over the next weeks, without forcing it.
- Approved the base block (27 → 30 → 32 → 28 → 30 km) and the 16-week plan, with one condition: the lighter week every fourth week is non-negotiable.
- Told her about the low vitamin D ([[Blood test 2026-09]]); she was glad I'm taking it.
