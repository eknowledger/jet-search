## How I use it

- **5 bpm or more above normal in the morning** → easy day only, whatever the plan says. It usually means a cold coming, a bad night or too much wine.
- It's the base for my heart-rate zones: with a max of 186, the reserve is 132 → zones in [[Paces and zones]].
- The trend over months matters more than any single morning.
