# Sourdough country loaf

My everyday bread: a pain de campagne style loaf with a little rye, about 900 g. The clock says 24 hours, but the hands-on time is around 40 minutes.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Grams | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| T65 white bread flour | 450 g | 90% |
| Wholemeal rye flour | 50 g | 10% |
| Water | 375 g | 75% |
| Levain, ripe | 100 g | 20% |
| Salt | 10 g | 2% |

**Levain build (Friday 21:00):** 10 g [[Sourdough starter — Fermento|Fermento]] + 50 g flour + 50 g water, 1:5:5, at 26 °C overnight. Ready around 06:00–07:00.

## Method

1. **Autolyse (Saturday 07:00).** Mix both flours with 350 g of the water. Cover and leave 45 minutes.
2. **Mix (07:45).** Add the 100 g levain and squeeze it through the dough. After 20 minutes add the salt with the last 25 g of water. Pinch and fold until it comes together again.
3. **Bulk (08:15 to about 12:30, at 25–26 °C).** Four sets of stretch and folds, 30 minutes apart, then leave it alone. Bulk is done when the dough has grown by about 50%, is domed and jiggly, with bubbles on the sides of the container.
4. **Pre-shape.** Tip out, round gently, bench rest 20–30 minutes uncovered.
5. **Shape.** A tight batard. Into a banneton dusted with rice flour, seam up.
6. **Cold retard.** Fridge at 4–5 °C for 14–16 hours.
7. **Bake (Sunday 07:00).** Preheat the oven with the Dutch oven inside at 250 °C for 45 minutes. Turn the loaf out, score one long cut at a shallow angle. Lid on, 20 minutes at 250 °C; lid off, 25 minutes at 230 °C, until deep brown.
8. **Cool.** At least one hour on a rack before cutting. Yes, really.

## Notes

- In summer, with the kitchen at 29 °C, bulk takes only 3 h 15 min. Watch the dough, not the clock.
- Flat loaf? Usually an over-fermented bulk or lazy shaping. Next time try 72% water.
- Seeded version: 80 g toasted sunflower and sesame seeds soaked in 80 g water, added at the last fold.
- Keeps 3 days in a linen bag. Freezes well sliced; toast straight from frozen.
- The crumb is more open with fresher flour. The bag from the mill in Mafra is better than supermarket T65.
