# Cooking

Everything I cook more than once ends up here. Each recipe is written in the language I learned it in: French from my grandmother, Spanish from Carmen, Japanese from cookbooks read with a dictionary next to the pan, Arabic from a cooking class in Beirut, Portuguese from Rui's mother. It keeps the recipes honest, and it doubles as reading practice for [[Languages]].

## From the terrace

From July to September the [[Rooftop Garden]] does half the shopping: San Marzano and Cherokee Purple tomatoes for the gazpacho, the ratatouille and 番茄炒蛋; basil, mint, parsley and chillies for everything else. The tomatoes are finished for this year; broad beans and garlic go in now.

## Rules I cook by

- Salt early, acid late.
- Taste before serving. Always.
- Write down what went wrong the same evening, while I still remember.
- If a recipe needs more than two pans, it had better be worth it.
