# Sourdough starter — Fermento

Fermento was born in July 2024, three weeks after [[Moving to Lisbon]], in a jam jar on the windowsill of the new flat. Rye flour and tap water, nothing else. He has survived two heat waves, one week of complete neglect and a trip to Porto in a cool bag.

## The basics

- **Ratio:** fed **1:5:5**, meaning 1 part starter, 5 parts flour, 5 parts water, by weight.
- **Temperature:** **26 °C**. At that temperature a 1:5:5 feed peaks in about 8–9 hours.
- **Flour:** 80% T65 white bread flour, 20% wholemeal rye. The rye keeps him lively.
- **Water:** filtered; around 28 °C in winter, straight from the tap in summer.
- **Jar:** 500 ml straight-sided glass jar, with a rubber band to mark the level after each feed.

A normal feed: 10 g Fermento + 50 g flour (40 g T65, 10 g rye) + 50 g water = 110 g. Keep 10 g for the next feed; bake with the rest or use it in pancakes.

## Schedule

When I'm baking every week:

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 08:00 | Feed 1:5:5, mark the jar |
| 16:00–17:00 | Peak: doubled, domed, full of bubbles, smells like yoghurt |
| 21:00 | Feed again 1:5:5; he peaks overnight, around 06:00 |

For a bake, the levain is built on Friday at 21:00, see [[Sourdough country loaf]].

**Fridge mode** (now, during the [[Kitchen Renovation]]): feed 1:5:5 on Sunday morning, leave him out for 2 hours, then into the fridge at 5 °C. Before baking again: two warm feeds at 26 °C, 12 hours apart.

**Getting 26 °C in winter:** the seedling heat mat with its thermostat, borrowed from the [[Rooftop Garden]] kit, with the jar wrapped in a tea towel. The oven with only the light on also does it, when we have an oven.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Grey liquid on top (hooch) | Hungry | Pour it off, feed 1:5:5 |
| Smells like nail polish remover | Very hungry | Two feeds 12 h apart at 26 °C |
| Slow, peaks after 14 h or more | Cold kitchen, or too much white flour | Warmer spot; raise the rye to 30% for a few days |
| Runny, no structure | Too warm or overripe | Feed a little stiffer, 1:5:4, for a day |
| Rises, then collapses fast | Peaked hours ago | Feed earlier, or cooler water |
| Pink or orange streaks | Wrong bacteria | Throw away; restart from the dried backup |
| Fuzzy mould spots | Contamination | Same: throw away, scald the jar, use the backup |
| Doesn't float | The float test is unreliable with rye | Trust the rise and the smell instead |

## Dried backup

Every spring I spread a spoonful of active starter very thin on baking paper, let it dry for two days and crumble it into a small jar. To revive: 10 g flakes + 20 g flour + 20 g water, then normal feeds from day 2. Last backup: 2026-04-12. Sofia keeps a jar too, just in case.
