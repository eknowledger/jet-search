# Cleaning routine

Split with [[Rui]] since the argument of March 2025. The rule: whoever cooks doesn't wash up, and nobody leaves the bins for the other one. We swap the weekly list every month so nobody gets the bathroom forever.

## Every day

- Pimenta's litter — whoever is up first
- Dishes in the dishwasher before bed; run it when full
- Wipe the hob and the table
- Ten-minute reset of the living room before bed

## Weekly

| Task | November 2026 | Day |
|---|---|---|
| Bathroom (toilet, sink, shower, mirror) | Noa | Saturday |
| Floors — vacuum everywhere, mop the tiles | Rui | Saturday |
| Change the bed | Noa | Sunday |
| Bins and recycling out (glass, paper, plastic) | Rui | Tuesday and Friday |
| Fridge check — throw out what's dead | whoever does the Saturday shop | Saturday |
| Water the indoor plants | Noa | Sunday |
| Pimenta's bowls through the dishwasher | Rui | Wednesday |

## Monthly

- Dishwasher filter out and rinsed; salt and rinse aid if needed
- Washing machine: empty drum at 90 °C with a cup of citric acid
- Wipe the fridge seal (it's going — on [[Things to fix]])
- Dust the shelves, the tops of the frames, the Pi and its UPS
- Clean the shower drain before it turns into an [[Unclog a sink]] evening
- Coffee machine: descale every second month — [[Descale the coffee machine]]

## Seasonal

- **Spring:** wash windows inside and out (as far as we can reach); the terrace furniture
- **Summer:** behind the fridge and the washing machine; the extractor fan filter
- **Autumn:** bleed the radiators before the [[Boiler service]]; check the [[Bedroom window leak]] towels are ready
- **Winter:** the dehumidifier filter every two weeks

## During the renovation

The dust gets everywhere, even with the plastic sheets. Until the [[Kitchen Renovation]] is done:

- Vacuum every two days, not weekly
- The bedroom door stays closed so Pimenta has one clean room
- Wipe the hallway floor in the evening after the crew leaves

## Things that don't go on the floor

The old terracotta kitchen tiles: no bleach, no acid cleaners. Neutral soap and water only. Ask Luis what to use on them after the renovation.
