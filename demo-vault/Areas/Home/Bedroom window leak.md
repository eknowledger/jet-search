# Bedroom window leak

The window in the bedroom leaks **when the rain comes from the south**. Rain from the north or straight down: nothing. A southerly or south-westerly storm with wind: water along the bottom of the frame, mostly the left corner, and a damp patch on the wall under it.

## What it is

- Old timber casement, painted over many times, on the south face of the building. Probably original to the 1930s.
- The putty on the outside of the lower pane is cracked and the drip groove under the opening sash is blocked with paint.
- The sill slopes slightly *inwards*. That's the real problem.

## When it happened

| Date | Rain | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| 2024-11-13 | southerly storm | first noticed — puddle on the floor, towel ruined |
| 2025-01-28 | south-west, strong wind | wall patch, about 20 cm |
| 2025-12-07 | storm from the south | water ran down to the skirting board |
| 2026-10-19 | south-west, all night | two towels; the dehumidifier ran for a day |

## What we do when the forecast says south

1. Rolled towel along the inside of the sill, a bowl under the left corner.
2. Move the books off the shelf under the window.
3. Dehumidifier on the next day (see [[Appliances]]).
4. Check the wall after 24 hours and take a photo for the landlord.

## The landlord

Sr. Almeida knows. E-mailed with photos on 2024-11-14, 2025-01-29 and 2025-12-08. Each time: "I'll send someone." Nobody came. Rui thinks we should send a formal letter this time.
