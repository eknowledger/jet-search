## How to top up the pressure

Shown by the technician in 2025, written down so I don't forget:

1. Boiler off, radiators cold.
2. Open the filling tap under the boiler (black lever) slowly.
3. Watch the gauge; stop at 1.2 bar.
4. Close the tap firmly. Boiler back on.
5. If it drops below 1 bar again within a week, there is a leak somewhere — call them.

## Why October

The first cold nights in Lisbon arrive in November; the technicians are fully booked by then. Also it's easy to remember: the boiler in October, the window leak in November, Christmas in December.
