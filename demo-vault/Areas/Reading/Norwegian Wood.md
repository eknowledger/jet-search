# Norwegian Wood

**Haruki Murakami** · to read · [[Book Club]] December pick (Tiago), meeting on 2026-12-03

[[Rui]] gave me his old copy — broken spine, his student-days underlining all through it — which is its own kind of book to read.

## Why it's on the list
- Book Club in December: Tiago's pick, the Christmas meeting at his place.
- Before the [[Japan Trip 2027]]: Tokyo in the late 1960s, students, a city I'll be walking through in March. I want to read it and then stand in Shinjuku and see what's left.
- I've only read Murakami's running memoir (*What I Talk About When I Talk About Running*), never the novels. A strange way round to come to a novelist.

## What I know going in
His most "realistic" novel — no talking cats, no wells. Toru Watanabe looks back on his student years, on Naoko, the girlfriend of his friend who died, and on the very different Midori. Everyone says it's sad. Rui says it's "sad in a very quiet way, like rain on a long train ride", which is the most romantic sentence he's said all year.
