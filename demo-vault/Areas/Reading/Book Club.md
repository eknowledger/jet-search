# Book Club

Seven of us. We meet on the **first Thursday of the month** at 19:30, at whoever's turn it is to host. August off. The host picks the book and feeds everyone, which is the real reason anyone comes.

## Members
- **Beatriz** — started it, in 2023. Reads everything twice.
- **Tiago** — only reads on the ferry across the river, so he likes books he can carry.
- **Joana** — always the first to have read it. And the ending, first.
- **Mariana** — brings the best wine and the worst opinions (her words).
- **Duarte** — non-fiction or nothing. Argues with authors in the margins.
- **Carla** — joined in January. Quiet until the second glass, then devastating.
- **Me.**

[[Rui]] isn't a member, but somehow always gets home just in time for dessert when we host.

## Rules
1. You can come without finishing. You can't come without an opinion.
2. No spoilers in the group chat before the meeting.
3. Everyone gives a score from 1 to 5 at the end; the average goes in the table.
4. Nothing over 500 pages unless everyone agrees in advance. (Broken once, for *The Overstory*. My fault.)
