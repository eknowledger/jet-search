## Rules I try to keep

1. Savings transfers go out on the 1st, before anything else.
2. Anything over €200 that isn't planned waits 48 hours.
3. Shared costs go through the joint account; personal stuff stays personal.
4. Subscriptions get reviewed every October.
5. Look at the numbers once a month, not every day.
