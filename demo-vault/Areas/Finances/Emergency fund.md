# Emergency fund

**Goal: €12,000. Now: €8,400** (70%). €300 goes in on the 1st of every month.

## Why €12,000

Six months of essentials in the worst case — if I had to cover the whole flat on my own for a while. The full shared column of the [[Monthly budget]] is €1,915, plus phone, transport and health, so roughly €2,000 a month × 6.

## Where it lives

A separate savings account, not the joint one, with instant access. It pays 2.1% gross, which is about 1.5% after the 28% tax on interest. That's roughly inflation, so in real terms the money stands still. That's fine: this pot is for access, not growth (see [[How compound interest works]]).

## What counts as an emergency

- Health: anything for me, [[Rui]] or Pimenta that can't wait
- The flat: the boiler dies, a leak that the landlord won't fix in time
- Losing income
- Getting home fast if family needs me in Porto

**Not** an emergency: Japan, a sale on lenses, the kitchen running over. The [[Kitchen Renovation]] is €4,240 over its €24,000 budget, and that gap comes from our general savings account — this fund stays at €8,400.
