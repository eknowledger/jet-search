# Savings rate

**22%** of take-home, averaged over January–September 2026. In a normal month: €638 saved of €2,900.

## How I count it

> saving ÷ take-home, per month

Saving = [[Emergency fund]] (€300) + [[Japan fund]] (€150) + index fund (€188). Withdrawals from the emergency fund count as negative saving that month. Money set aside for something I'm about to spend (the kitchen pot) does **not** count — it's spending in instalments.

I keep it simple on purpose. Some people count pension contributions or debt repayments; I don't have the second and I track the first elsewhere.

## Is 22% good?

Good enough for this year. The usual advice is 20% and the people online who save 50% have very different lives. What matters more to me is that the rate is *steady*, because the money that matters — the index fund — grows mostly with time (see [[How compound interest works]]).
