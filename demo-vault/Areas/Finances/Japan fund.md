- Trip budget: **€6,500 for two** → my half €3,250
- Flights: €1,140 per person, booked 2026-09-20 — paid from the current account, not the fund
- What the fund still has to cover: €3,250 − €1,140 = **€2,110**
- Balance after the October transfer: **€1,500** (10 × €150)
- By March 2027: 15 transfers = €2,250 → about **€140 spare**, earmarked for a knife in Kyoto (see [[Sharpen a kitchen knife]])

## Rules for the money

- Pay with the card that has no foreign-exchange fee; take cash out at 7-Eleven ATMs.
- If the yen weakens past ¥175, top up the pocket early and lock the rate.
- Whatever is left after the trip goes into the [[Emergency fund]], not back to spending.
- [[Kenji Mori]] asks for a cash deposit at the ryokan on arrival — set aside ¥20,000 in notes.
