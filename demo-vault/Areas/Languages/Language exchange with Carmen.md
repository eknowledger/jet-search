# Language exchange with Carmen

A weekly tandem with Carmen. She's from Seville, moved to Lisbon in 2025 and is learning Portuguese; I'm preparing for the DELE B2 in May 2027 ([[Languages]]). We met at a language café in Graça last winter and have kept going since.

## How it goes

- **When:** Tuesdays, 19:00, at a small café near the Graça viewpoint. About 90 minutes.
- **Split:** 45 minutes in Spanish, 45 in Portuguese. A coin decides which comes first.
- **Rules:** each of us brings one topic. Corrections only after the other person finishes a thought. Both of us write down three new words at the end.
- **Every fourth week** we cook together instead. That's how [[Tortilla de patatas]] and [[Gazpacho andaluz]] got into my recipes.

## DELE plan with Carmen

- [ ] From January: a monthly mock oral exam, 20 minutes, after the 20 minutes of preparation, with her timing me.
- [ ] She marks my Saturday writing texts (letters, opinion articles, reviews).
- [x] Swap reading lists: she gave me *La sombra del viento*; I gave her *O Ano da Morte de Ricardo Reis* (Saramago), which she says is "very Portuguese and very long".

## My repeated mistakes

1. Future instead of present subjunctive after *cuando*.
2. *Exquisito* versus *esquisito*.
3. Gender that differs from Portuguese: *la leche* (o leite), *la sal* (o sal), *el puente* (a ponte), *el árbol* (a árvore), *la sangre* (o sangue).
4. *Me gusta* agreement: *me gustan los tomates*, not *me gusta los tomates*.
5. Using *ya* everywhere, like Portuguese *já*.
