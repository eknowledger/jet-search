## 似ている文字: look-alikes

| Characters | Trick |
|---|---|
| シ shi / ツ tsu | シ: the dots are on the left and the stroke goes up; ツ: dots on top, stroke goes down |
| ソ so / ン n | same idea: ソ falls from the top, ン rises from the bottom |
| ク ku / ケ ke / タ ta | タ is ク with an extra line inside |
| ウ u / ワ wa / フ fu | ウ has the hat, ワ has the step, フ has neither |
| ノ no / メ me / ヌ nu | ノ is one stroke, メ two, ヌ has the hook |
| チ chi / テ te | チ crosses, テ doesn't |
| ユ yu / コ ko | ユ is open on the left |

## Small and long

- ー is the long vowel mark: コーヒー kōhī, ビール bīru.
- Small ッ doubles: コップ koppu, チケット chiketto.
- Small ャ ュ ョ: ジュース jūsu, チョコ choko.
- New combinations only in katakana: ティ (ティー, tea), ファ (ファミリーマート), ウィ (ウィーン, Vienna), ヴ (ヴァイオリン).

## Words that came from Portuguese

My favourite way to practise: Japanese words that came from Portuguese in the 16th century.

| カタカナ | Romaji | From | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| パン | pan | pão | bread |
| カステラ | kasutera | pão de Castela | sponge cake |
| ボタン | botan | botão | button |
| タバコ | tabako | tabaco | cigarette |
| カルタ | karuta | carta | playing cards |
| ジョウロ | jōro | jorro | watering can (useful for the terrace) |
| コンペイトウ | konpeitō | confeito | sugar candy |
| オルガン | orugan | órgão | organ |

Some say テンプラ (usually written 天ぷら) comes from Portuguese too, from "têmporas". Not everyone agrees.
