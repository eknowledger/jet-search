## Habits

- Write the roll number on the canister with a marker before loading.
- Note the date and subject here the day the roll finishes, not later.
- Negatives in archival sleeves, one page per roll, in the blue binder on the bookshelf.
- Scans go to the "Film 2026" folder on the [[Home Server]], named `2026-14-05.jpg` (year, roll, frame).
