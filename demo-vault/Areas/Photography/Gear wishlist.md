# Gear wishlist

Two rules. Nothing goes on the list unless I missed it on a real walk at least twice. Nothing gets bought unless it's still on the list a month later. Photography money: €40 a month from the fun budget ([[Finances]]).

## Before Japan (by March 2027)

- [ ] **2 spare NP-W235 batteries**, about €60 each. The X-T5 died during blue hour on the [[Alfama photo walk — October]]. Priority 1.
- [ ] **1 more roll of Portra 400**, about €19, to have 5 for the trip ([[Film rolls log]]). Priority 1.
- [ ] **Travel tripod**: under 1.5 kg, fits inside the backpack. The Peak Design Travel (about €380) or a cheaper Sirui (about €180). Priority 2. See [[Night photography attempts]].
- [ ] **A small sling bag** that holds one camera and two rolls, about €50.
- [ ] Ask for hand checks of film at airport security instead of buying a lead bag. Note the phrase in Japanese for the trip ([[Japan Trip 2027]]).

## Maybe

- [ ] **Fujinon XF 50 mm f/2**, about €450. 75 mm equivalent, for portraits and temple details. Read [[Lens guide — primes vs zooms]] again first.
- [ ] **XF 30 mm f/2.8 Macro**, about €700, to scan negatives with the camera: much faster than the flatbed. Expensive for a scanner.
- [ ] **Zuiko 28 mm f/2.8** for the OM-1, €60–90 used, if I get lucky at the Feira da Ladra. Better than the 50 mm for zone focusing on film ([[Zone focusing for street photography]]).
- [ ] **43 mm variable ND filter**, about €60. Slow shutter speeds for the river in daylight.
