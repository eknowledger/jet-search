# Alfama photo walk — October

Saturday 2026-10-17, in two parts: morning (07:30–10:00) and evening (17:45–19:30). Sunrise 07:48, sunset 18:56. Clear sky, a little haze over the river in the morning, warm for October.

Cameras: the X-T5 with the 23 mm on Lisbon Gold ([[Film simulation recipes]]) and the OM-1 with roll 14, Portra 400.

The goal: photos for the [[Photo book for Sofia]], especially the morning and evening chapters, which were thin.

## Route

1. **Sé**, the cathedral. Tram 28 passing in front of the façade at 07:50, first light on the towers.
2. **Miradouro de Santa Luzia.** Tiles, bougainvillea, the river beyond.
3. **Largo das Portas do Sol.** The classic view over the rooftops to São Vicente. Already crowded by 09:30.
4. **Beco do Carneiro**, the narrowest alley. Zone focused at 2 m, f/8 ([[Zone focusing for street photography]]).
5. **Igreja de São Miguel** and the stairs below: laundry lines, a man carrying a bag of bread rolls.
6. **Largo do Chafariz de Dentro.** Coffee and a pastel de nata, then twenty minutes of people-watching.
7. Evening: the last stalls packing up at the **Feira da Ladra**, the **Panteão** at golden hour, then back through **Rua dos Remédios** at blue hour.

## Settings

- Morning, in the shade: f/5.6, 1/250, Auto ISO (400–1600).
- Golden hour: f/4, 1/500, ISO 200, −1/3 to keep the highlights on the walls.
- Blue hour: f/2, 1/30 handheld, ISO 3200–6400.
- Film: Portra 400 at box speed, metered for the shadows (one stop over in the alleys).

Favourites:

- Tram 28 at Portas do Sol at 1/15, panned. One sharp frame out of twelve tries.
- Laundry against the river at golden hour, on Portra. Maybe the cover.

## Lessons

- 07:30 is the right time. By 09:30 the tour groups own Portas do Sol.
- I asked before photographing faces. Two people said no; one said yes and asked for a print. I owe her one: Rua de São Pedro, the green door.
- Forgot the spare battery. The X-T5 died at 19:10, just as blue hour got good. → [[Gear wishlist]]
- Golden hour is short in October; the light on the Panteão lasted about 20 minutes. Be there early ([[Golden hour and blue hour]]).
