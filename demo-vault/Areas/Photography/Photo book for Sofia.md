# Photo book for Sofia

A photo book of Alfama for [[Sofia]]'s birthday on **2027-02-11**. She walked us through Alfama the weekend we came to look at flats in 2024 ([[Moving to Lisbon]]), and she still says she chose our street. It's a surprise: only [[Rui]] knows.

## Concept

**"Alfama, de manhã à noite"**: one day in the neighbourhood, built from a year of walks.

1. **Manhã:** first light, bread, trams, laundry.
2. **Tarde:** markets, stairs, tiles, cats.
3. **Noite:** street lamps, fado houses, blue hour over the river.

The last spread: Pimenta on our windowsill on the left, and on the right Sofia and me at her wedding in 2025 ([[Sofia's Wedding Speech]]).

## Sequencing rules

- Alternate wide and close.
- No two trams on facing pages.
- Film and digital can mix in the book, but each spread is one or the other.
- One empty page before each chapter, with only the chapter's name.
- Faces only with permission.
