# Photography

Mostly street and everyday life in Alfama, on two cameras: a digital one for every day, and a film camera for when I want to slow down.

## Cameras

| Camera | Lens | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Fujifilm **X-T5** | XF **23 mm f/2** (about 35 mm equivalent) | Everyday, walks, night |
| Olympus **OM-1** (the 1970s film one) | Zuiko 50 mm f/1.8 | Film, almost always loaded with **Portra 400** |

The OM-1 came from a stall at the Feira da Ladra in 2023 for €90, with a sticky shutter that a repair shop in Baixa fixed for €60.

Favourite film-simulation recipe: **"Lisbon Gold"**: Classic Chrome, WB 5200 K, +2 red. All the settings are in [[Film simulation recipes]].

## Habits

- One walk a week, even a short one. Saturday mornings before the city wakes up, or the last hour of light.
- Cull the same evening: flag the keepers, delete the rest without mercy.
- Keepers go to the "Alfama book" album on the [[Home Server]]. The photo library, about 120 GB, is backed up every night.
- One roll of film a month. Developing at home since August: [[Developing C-41 at home]].
