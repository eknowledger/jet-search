# Inês Costa

**Role:** physiotherapist, sports injuries and runners in particular
**Area:** [[Health]] · **Project:** [[Half Marathon]]

## How I know her

Recommended by someone from the Sunday running group in Monsanto when my shins started hurting in September 2026. Her clinic is in Graça, a ten-minute walk uphill from the flat, which she says counts as a warm-up.

## The shin splints, September 2026

- Pain along the inside of both shins, worse on downhill runs and on the cobbles in Alfama
- Her diagnosis: **shin splints**, from too many kilometres added too fast in August, plus old shoes
- Prescription: **2 weeks of complete rest from running**, then a slow return
- Every day: **calf raises 3 × 15**, slow down, on the edge of a step
- Also: hip and glute exercises twice a week, foam roller on the calves

> "Your shins aren't weak. Your calves are lazy and your shins are carrying the load for them."

The 2 weeks felt endless. The calf raises are now part of brushing my teeth.

## What she said about the race

- The **16-week plan from 2026-11-16** is fine, as long as the long run grows slowly, never more than 10% a week
- No back-to-back hard days; easy really means easy
- Shoes: the Pegasus have 420 km on them; she wants them out well before 600 km, and a second pair for long runs ([[Half Marathon]])
- If the pain comes back at the same spot: stop, ice, call her, don't "run through it"

## Contact

- Bookings by phone with the clinic's front desk or through their online booking page
- Between sessions she answers short questions by text, usually in the evening
- Sessions: 45 minutes; bring running shoes and shorts
- Receipts go into the health folder for the tax return ([[Finances]])
