# Marta Silva

**Role:** plumber, independent, her own van and her own very organised toolboxes
**Project:** [[Kitchen Renovation]]

## How I know her

[[Luis Ortega]] brought her in; they have done kitchens together for years and finish each other's sentences about pipes. She came on 2026-09-28 to look at the sink move before the works started, and was the second person to say she didn't like the smell under the old sink. She was right.

## What she does in our kitchen

- Moves the sink **60 cm** towards the window
- New hot and cold supply with isolation valves, waste extended with a proper fall
- A separate valve for the dishwasher
- Caps the old gas line at the meter (we went induction, see [[Decision — induction or gas]])
- Comes back after the countertop is fitted to connect the tap and waste ([[Countertop — quartz]])

Details: [[Electrics and plumbing]].

## The joist

On Tuesday 2026-10-13 she lifted the old sink base and the tiles in the corner, pushed a screwdriver into the joist and it went in to the handle. Without her we would have put new oak cabinets on top of a rotten floor. €950 to fix, approved the same evening. The whole story: [[Rotten joist under the sink]].

> "Old sinks always leak a little. For years. Nobody sees it."
