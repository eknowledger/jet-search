# Luis Ortega

**Role:** contractor, runs **Ortega & Filhos** with his two sons
**Project:** [[Kitchen Renovation]]

## How I know him

The neighbour on the 2nd floor had her bathroom redone by Ortega & Filhos last year and would not stop talking about it. Luis came to see our kitchen on 2026-08-13, stayed almost an hour, and was the only one of three contractors who looked under the sink. We chose his quote on 2026-08-30: see [[Quote comparison]].

## The deal

- **€18,750** for labour and materials, everything except the cabinets and the countertop
- Two-year written guarantee on labour
- Works started **2026-10-05**, target finish **2026-12-14**
- Site meeting every **Monday at 8:30**, in the kitchen
- Payments in four stages, details in [[Budget tracker]]

## What he's like

- Early. Always ten minutes early, usually with coffees.
- Explains things with his hands and a pencil on the bare wall.
- Calm when something goes wrong. When the [[Rotten joist under the sink]] turned up he gave us three options with prices within an hour and recommended the middle one (€950).
- Doesn't like surprises in money either: every extra in writing before anyone starts.
- Has strong opinions on lime mortar ("cement kills old floors"), which is exactly why the terracotta is still there ([[Floor tiles]]).

> "In an old building, the wall tells you what it wants. You just have to open it first."
