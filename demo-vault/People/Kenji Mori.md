# Kenji Mori

**Role:** host of **Mori-ya**, a small family ryokan in Kyoto
**Project:** [[Japan Trip 2027]]

## How I know him

Only by email so far. I found Mori-ya while reading about ryokans that still serve dinner in the room, and wrote to ask about spring. He answered the same night, in careful English, with a hand-drawn map of the street attached. We have written back and forth since then, a little more Japanese in each of my emails.

## What he told us

- The cherry blossom forecast for Kyoto says **full bloom around April 3**, and the town will be busy. Book everything else early.
- Go to **Fushimi Inari at 6 am**: "before the tour buses, only the cats and the crows."
- Dinner is at 18:30 sharp; tell him about any food we don't eat at least a week before.
- Luggage: send the big suitcases ahead from Tokyo by delivery service, arrive with a small bag only.
- Shoes off at the entrance, slippers inside, no slippers on the tatami.

## Japanese for our emails

My emails end with a line he corrects kindly (see [[Languages]]):

- よろしくお願いします。 (yoroshiku onegaishimasu): "I look forward to it / please treat us well"
- ありがとうございます。 (arigatou gozaimasu): "thank you very much"
- 春にお会いできるのを楽しみにしています。: "I'm looking forward to meeting you in spring"

He wrote back: 「とても上手です！」 I doubt it, but I'm keeping the email.

## Contact

- Email only, through the address on the ryokan's booking page; the whole thread is saved with the trip notes
