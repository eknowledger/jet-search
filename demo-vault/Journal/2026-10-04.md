Sowed the winter crops on the [[Rooftop Garden]]. Broad beans in the long trough along the parapet, two rows, about 5 cm deep and a hand apart; garlic cloves pointy end up in the deep pot by the door. The terrace gets the south-west wind full on, so the beans will need some string later.

The fleece for the lemon tree is folded in the cupboard. Not needed yet; nights are still around 17 °C. The basil is hanging on in its terracotta pot, watered only when the top 2 cm are dry. I am trying to be strict.

Last quiet Sunday before demolition. Revision done before breakfast, then a long, slow coffee in the sun with [[Rui]].
