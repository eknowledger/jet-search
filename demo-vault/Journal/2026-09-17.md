The calf raises stay, every day. The plan from here: build back slowly, nothing long until October, and then the real 16 weeks start on 16 November for the Lisbon Half on 7 March. Goal 1:45:00, which means 4:58 per km, which today felt like science fiction. My best is 1:53:40, from last year.
