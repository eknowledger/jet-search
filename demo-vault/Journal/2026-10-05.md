Day one. First site meeting at 8:30 with [[Luis Ortega]] in what used to be the kitchen, both of us holding coffee from the camp hob. Plan for the week: demolition, all of it. By half past nine there were three men with crowbars and a skip in the street, and by lunch the old cabinets were in it.

The floor is the part I care about: the 1930s terracotta, 20 × 20 tiles. Luis is lifting the ones around the sink by hand so they can go back down, and the 2 spare boxes are our insurance.

Pimenta spent the whole day under the bed. I spent it at the library with the novel, which is a kind of hiding too.
