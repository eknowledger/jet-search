[[Inês Costa]] pressed along both shins for ten seconds and said "shin splints" before I'd finished explaining. Too much distance too soon after the summer, plus hills. Her prescription:

- **2 weeks rest** from running (walking and swimming are fine)
- calf raises **3×15 daily**, slow on the way down
- ice after long days on my feet
- come back if the stairs still hurt after ten days

Two weeks feels like forever, but the race is 7 March and the proper 16-week plan doesn't start until 16 November, so this is the cheapest possible moment to be injured. That's what I'm telling myself, anyway.
