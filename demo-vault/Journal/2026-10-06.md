Dust on everything, even inside the bread tin, which lives in the bedroom now. Escaped to a café near the Portas do Sol viewpoint and wrote my 600 words for [[The Lighthouse Keeper]] with the 28 tram grinding past every few minutes. Elena counts the fishing boats from the gallery each morning, and today one didn't come back. It's small, but it's the first crack in the island.

Home at four: the builders had gone and the silence was enormous. Pimenta came out from under the bed for exactly one minute.
