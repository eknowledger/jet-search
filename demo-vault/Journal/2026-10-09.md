Six tiles cracked, and the 2 spare boxes cover that easily. [[Luis Ortega]] stacked the good ones in the hallway like treasure, which they are.

Shot a roll of Portra 400 on the OM-1 of the empty room: dust in the light from the window, Luis's pencil marks on the wall, the ghost outline of the old sink. Not for anything, just for us. [[Kitchen Renovation]], [[Photography]]
