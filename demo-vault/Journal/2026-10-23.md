First proper autumn storm: wind banging the terrace door all night and a forecast of 11 °C for tonight. Below 12 °C is the rule, so the basil came inside in its terracotta pot and now lives on the living-room windowsill next to Pimenta, who has opinions about it.

Before the rain I checked the trough: the broad beans are up! A row of little green fists, nearly every one. The garlic has thin shoots too. Tied the first string along the beans so the wind doesn't flatten them, and wrapped the lemon tree in its fleece a few weeks early. Better early. [[Rooftop Garden]]
