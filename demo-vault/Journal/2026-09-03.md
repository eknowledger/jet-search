Decision made: **induction**, not gas. [[Luis Ortega]] came by at nine with his tape measure and we went through it one last time standing at the old stove. In the end it wasn't close:

- air quality: no more burning gas in a small kitchen with one window
- the building's old gas pipes, which Luis didn't like the look of at all
- a gas hob would need a **€480** gas inspection before anyone signs it off

The catch: the electrician has to run a dedicated **32 A circuit** for the hob. Luis says it fits in weeks 2–3 with the plumbing. [[Rui]] is mourning the blue flame for his tortillas; I told him they make woks for induction.
